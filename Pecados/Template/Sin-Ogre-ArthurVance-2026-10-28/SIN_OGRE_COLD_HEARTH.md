## 1. Lore & Manifestation Background

### Inciting Incident & Host Relationship
Arthur Vance spent 31 years tending the subterranean coal boilers heating district IV of Old Aethelgard. When the municipal council privatized energy distribution and abruptly decommissioned the plant in mid-winter, Vance and forty laborers were locked out without severance or pensions. Over the subsequent two years, Vance watched his diabetic wife succumb to hypothermia and medical neglect in their freezing tenement flat. Sinking into overwhelming guilt, Vance blamed his own compliance and inability to heat their home.

The Ogre coalesced inside Vance's chest as an aching, frostbitten void that feeds on his conviction of total failure. Rather than acting as an external parasite, the Sin lives in a co-dependent symbiosis with Vance: it soothes his unbearable sorrow with an icy, narcotic stupor while physically anchoring him inside the flooded lower maintenance corridors of the dead boiler station. Vance believes the cold is a mercy that preserves his wife's memory.

### Appearance & Psychophysiology
The Cold Hearth stands roughly nine feet tall, a hunchbacked mountain of necrotic, frost-rimed fat, slub-iron furnace plates fused to skin, and calcified coal clinkers. Its head is sunken into its clavicles, possessing no neck; its mouth is a jagged furnace hatch oozing black, sub-zero sludge that freezes on contact with concrete. Ambient temperature drops 25°C within twenty paces of it. Frost crawls across walls in jagged fractal patterns, accompanied by the muffled subterranean rattle of rattling pipes and whistling vents.

### Behavior & Combat Psychology
Outside of combat, The Cold Hearth is reclusive and deeply conflict-averse. It crouches in flooded basements among rusted piping, muttering fragmented boiler pressure logs through Vance's vocal cords. It desires only to remain undisturbed in its frozen slumber. If approached by graceless humans, its presence induces a weeping lethargy that rapidly renders witnesses catatonic. When cornered by Exorcists, it fights with panicked, lumbering savagery, smashing concrete columns and attempting to bury its assailants beneath freezing sludge before retreating into the dark.

### Specific Sin Limitations & Weaknesses
- **Conflict Averse**: The Cold Hearth will not actively pursue Exorcists outside its Miasma zone unless attacked.
- **Extreme Fire / Chemical Vulnerability**: Takes standard damage from psychic pyres (Ardence) and chemical fire; cannot regenerate segments lost to thermal attacks for the remainder of the scene.
- **Photophobia**: In areas illuminated by intense, sustained electric light or flare projectors, its movement is hindered and its camouflage in darkness is completely negated.

## 2. Traumas (The Three Canonical Questions)

> *Rule: These three questions must NEVER be modified in wording. Uncovering the truthful answer during the hunt allows Exorcists to **Counter** a reaction once per question, rolling 1d3 to reduce stress and deal that many slashes to the Execution Talisman.*

1. **Who or what pushed you into this hole?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: Municipal Commissioner Julian Sterling, who falsified the plant's structural safety inspection reports to justify immediate closure without paying municipal severance guarantees.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: Shredded administrative union records and waterlogged ledgers in the boiler plant's second-floor overseer office bearing Sterling's forged seal.

2. **Who or what is keeping you from going over the edge?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: A silver wedding locket tucked inside a pocket watch on Vance's chest containing a photograph of his late wife Clara and a handwritten winter recipe for spiced broth.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: An empty pawn shop receipt found in Vance's tenement room showing he sold all his tools and clothes to redeem that single locket three days before disappearing into the plant.

3. **What are you most ashamed of?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: On the night Clara froze to death, Arthur burned her remaining hand-knitted blankets in the kitchen stove to keep himself warm instead of wrapping them around her shivering body.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: Charred wool remnants in the grate of Vance's tenement stove, alongside an unopened bottle of paraffin oil he was too terrified of igniting.

## 3. Palace (The Pocket Dimension)

- **Location in Investigation Zone**: Hidden behind the rusted steel bulkhead of Boiler Unit #4 in the flooded sub-basement of the municipal heating plant (accessible only by wading through knee-deep icy sludge).
- **Architectural Manifestation**: The interior expands into an infinite, labyrinthine subterranean factory of towering rusted iron boilers, dripping pipe networks, and staircases that lead upside-down into frozen reservoirs. Thick icicles of black sludge hang from iron gantries.
- **Atmospheric Sensory Tags**: *Dark, Wet, Cold, Musty, Reeking, Filthy.*
- **Palace Rules**:
  - *Outside Palace*: If The Cold Hearth takes 4 slashes or is reduced to 0 slashes outside its Palace, it dissolves into freezing black water and retreats to Boiler Unit #4 until Pressure increases, healing 1 (or 2d3 if full). It can **never** be executed outside its Palace.
  - *Inside Palace*: Fighting here initiates a climactic Execution Scene where the Sin cannot flee.

- **Pressure Talisman**: `[ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ]` (6 segments; advances when Tension overflows)

### Escalation Track (0 to 6)
- **Pressure 0-2 (Localized Frost)**: Rime coats building facades within 3 blocks of the plant. Ambient temperatures hover near freezing. Car batteries die prematurely; landlines produce wet static.
- **Pressure 3-4 (Creeping Rot & Mold)**: Dense, foul-smelling gray fog rolls across the district. A thick white mold spreads across asphalt, brickwork, and electronic circuit boards, causing power transformers to burst.
- **Pressure 5 (Urban Paralysis)**: Heating infrastructure across four neighborhoods fails. Freezing rain coats roads in black ice. Architecture becomes distorted; alleyways loop back into themselves. Civilians become despondent, aggressive, and barricade themselves in cold rooms.
- **Pressure 6+ (Out of Control)**:
  - The Cold Hearth increases in Category to **CAT 4** (Execution Talisman expands to 10 segments).
  - The Miasma covers the **entire municipal metropolitan area**. The freezing black fog follows the Exorcists everywhere they travel for the remainder of the mission, even if they attempt exfiltration.

## 5. The Three Chosen Domains

### Domain 1: Frozen Labyrinth of the Lockout
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[01_I_Ogre#1. Hostile Door Patterns|1. Hostile Door Patterns]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: Steam pipes snap and weld themselves across doorways; frost freezes lock cylinders shut; familiar staircases plunge into pitch-black industrial catacombs.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - *Complication / Tension Move*: The Sin supernaturally erases entrances, exits, vehicles, or streetlights in an area of one city block. These return when the scene passes or the complication is resolved.
  - *Once a Hunt Tension Move*: If an Exorcist opens any door, the entire cell is dragged into a shifting maze of frozen steam pipes and dripping corridors. Escaping requires 1-2 scenes of dedicated navigation rolls.

### Domain 2: Sludge of the Dead Hearth
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[01_I_Ogre#4. That Awful Flesh|4. That Awful Flesh]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: Frostbitten blubber and slag re-congeal across its torso; wounds ooze sub-zero oil that crusts into protective black rime.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - *Regeneration*: Regenerates 1 segment on its Execution Talisman every time a risk result of '1' is rolled in a conflict scene where it is present.
  - *Resilience*: Takes **-1 slash** on its Execution Talisman unless damaged by fire, acid, or powerful solvents in the same scene.

### Domain 3: The Chill of Living Ash
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[01_I_Ogre#7. Where You Belong|7. Where You Belong]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: Melting through slush and pools of freezing bilge water, re-emerging soundlessly from bursting radiators or storm drains behind targets.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - *Bilge Step*: Can sink into any pool of sludge or water and reappear within short distance as part of any reaction.
  - *Tension Move (Weather Shift)*: The Sin plunges the local weather into an arctic blizzard until the Exorcists rest. All outdoor activities requiring manual dexterity, concentration, or aiming become **Hard** and **Risky** without thermal protective gear.

## 6. Combat Profile & Execution Talisman

### Execution Talisman: 9 Segments (6 + CAT 3)

### Reaction Die Ladder (Derived from Opponent's Risk Die)
- **Result 1**: 3 Stress (or major Complication / Threat)
- **Result 2-3**: 2 Stress (or Complication / Threat)
- **Result 4-6**: 1 Stress

### Attacks & Armament
- **Attacks with**: Slag-encrusted fists, iron furnace grate teeth, pressurized sub-zero sludge, flailing rusted boiler chains.

### Threats (1 per round)
1. *Snatch an Exorcist in an iron-hard grip and freeze their torso against the cold furnace belly.*
2. *Rupture high-pressure steam pipes to collapse brick walls and scald the arena.*
3. *Hurl an Exorcist through frozen scaffolding into an open industrial coal hopper.*
4. *Summon 1d3 Stumblers from pools of black runoff.*
5. *Extinguish all mobile light sources in a blast of freezing soot.*

### Complications (Max 3 per conflict scene)
1. *Submerge the floor under knee-deep freezing sludge (movement requires 1 stress).*
2. *Emit an acrid cloud of soot and ammonia mold (obscures sight; ranged actions Hard).*
3. *Slag Armor: Slush freezes into thick rind (takes -1 slash until shattered).*
4. *Barricade exit behind twisted rusted pipes (separates the arena).*

### Severe Attack: Pulverize (Crushing Cold)
- **Trigger**: Result of '1' on an Exorcist's Risk Die (Usable once per hunt).
- **Participation**: Target exorcist and any allies who fly to their aid.
- **Dice Pool**: Starts at 5d6. Subtract 1 die for each affirmative answer:
  1. *Is another person aiding you?*
  2. *Can you grab on to something sturdy nearby?*
  3. *Do you possess an active source of bright light or intense heat?*
  4. *Is The Cold Hearth distracted, hindered, or under duress?*
- **Resolution**:
  - Exorcist and all helpers take 1 stress per die rolled.
  - If at least one '1' is rolled: Target takes an **Injury** and the **Mangled Limbs** affliction (*all physical activity is Hard unless set up by an ally*).
  - If two or more '1's are rolled: Target suffers a severed limb (roll 1d3: 1: arm, 2: leg, 3: both legs) and falls unconscious until the scene passes.

## 7. Traces (Minions)

### Stumblers (Slag Shamblers)
Bulbous humanoids composed of freezing soot, rusted metal scraps, and white mold, drawn to the warmth of living bodies.
- **Execution Talisman**: 2 (Solo) | 4 (Group) | 6 (Massive Horde)
- **Traits**:
  - Disintegrate rapidly outside the Miasma.
  - Slow and uncoordinated: Actions taking advantage of their sluggishness roll **+1D**.
  - Solid bulk: Take **-1 slash** from physical harm.
- **Reactions**:
  - *Inflict Stress*: Attacks with jagged scrap, blunt weight, frozen bile. (1): 3 stress, (2-3): 2 stress, (4+): 1 stress.
  - *Complications / Threats*: Smother in freezing mud, explode into shard-laden soot, bite and infect with white mold.

## 8. Specific Afflictions

1. **White Mold**: Coughing up pale fungal spores and black frost rime. Subtract 1 from all resting rolls; cannot consume rations or stimulants.
2. **Frozen Limbs**: Fingers and joints turn black and numb. Physical activity or manual dexterity is **Hard** if requiring more than one limb.
3. **Circling the Drain**: Mental despair takes root. Cannot benefit from teamwork or setup. Permanently add to agenda: **Give up on something.**
