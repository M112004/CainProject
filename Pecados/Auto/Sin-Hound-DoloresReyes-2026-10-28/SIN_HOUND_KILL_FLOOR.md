# SIN_HOUND_KILL_FLOOR

> **Codename / Designation**: Subject H-231 / "THE KILL FLOOR"  
> **Type**: III. HOUND  
> **Form**: Type II (Fused with Host)  
> **Category (CAT)**: CAT 3  
> **Primary Emotion**: Vengeance  
> **Host**: Dolores "Lola" Reyes (Age 44, line worker at the Harlan & Sons meatpacking plant for 19 years, widowed, now missing and presumed fled after the plant's "workplace incident")  
> **Canonical Reference**: [[03_III_Hound]] | [[Designing_Sins]]

## 1. Lore & Manifestation Background

### Inciting Incident & Host Relationship
Eleven months ago Lola's son Mateo (19, her only child) was killed on the Harlan & Sons deboning line when a conveyor interlock failed and drew him into the blade carriage. Plant manager Gordon Pruitt had ordered the interlock bypassed three weeks earlier to hit a holiday quota. Safety inspector Alma Thorne signed the quarterly audit without walking the floor. Company physician Dr. Wendell Cray then ruled the toxicology "consistent with impairment," and the plant's insurer denied the claim. Mateo's file now reads: drunk, careless, fired posthumously.

Lola spent eleven months writing letters, filing complaints, and standing outside the gates. Nobody answered. On the anniversary of his death she walked back onto the line during third shift and did not come out. The Hound did not tear itself out of her. It rose through her like a fever finally breaking. Lola is still conscious in there, a passenger watching her own hands work, and she does not want it to stop.

### Appearance & Psychophysiology
Roughly seven feet tall when it runs upright, and it rarely does. The Kill Floor goes on all fours, gaunt and skinned-looking, with plant-issue rubber apron fused into its back like a second hide. Boning knives grow from its forearms and knuckles, some still stamped with the Harlan & Sons logo. Its jaw unhinges sideways. The body runs hot enough to boil rain off its shoulders, and it breathes out a rusty steam that smells of bleach and offal. Anyone within sight of it, even through a wall, feels the primal prickle of being hunted.

### Behavior & Combat Psychology
Almost no speech. It makes a wet, rhythmic clicking that matches the sound of the line's conveyor, and sometimes a woman's voice rasps one word: "Mateo." It circles, it twitches, it waits when a target is guarded, and it explodes the instant a gap opens. It ignores anyone not on its list unless they stand in the way, then ignores them only after they stop moving. It goes quiet and still when shown anything of Mateo's.

### Specific Sin Limitations & Weaknesses
- **Single-Minded**: Its attention is on its three Grudge targets. Bait, decoys, and traps built around them draw it in reliably.
- **Predictable Rage**: Reckless charges can be channeled into prepared killing grounds. Setups against it are easy to justify.
- **Cold**: Extreme cold strips its supernatural resilience (see Domain 3). The industrial freezer where Mateo's body was held is its one great fear and its one great lever.
- **Least Communicative**: It cannot be argued with. It can only be hurt, led, or reminded.

## 2. Traumas (The Three Canonical Questions)

> *Rule: These three questions must NEVER be modified in wording. Uncovering the truthful answer during the hunt allows Exorcists to **Counter** a reaction once per question, rolling 1d3 to reduce stress and deal that many slashes to the Execution Talisman.*

1. **Who wronged you?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: Gordon Pruitt (plant manager), Alma Thorne (safety inspector), and Dr. Wendell Cray (company physician), the three people who killed Mateo and then buried it.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: Lola's wall of photocopied complaint letters in her kitchen, with three names circled in red marker and every reply stamped "NO ACTION."

2. **How were you wronged?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: Pruitt bypassed the line-4 interlock for quota, Thorne signed the audit blind, and Cray falsified the toxicology so the insurer would deny Mateo's death benefit.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: A jury-rigged jumper wire still clipped into the interlock panel, a quarterly audit with Thorne's signature dated a day she was on vacation, and a lab sheet whose sample ID does not match Mateo's.

3. **What are you unwilling to sacrifice?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: Mateo's name. She will give up her body, her life, and her mind, but not the truth of who he was and how he died. She will not let the record stand.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: His boxing medals polished and untouched in a drawer, a half-finished letter addressed to "whoever reads this after me" begging that his name be cleared, and a Harlan & Sons pay stub with "MATEO R." scratched out of the personnel board.

## 3. Palace (The Pocket Dimension)

- **Location in Investigation Zone**: Starts behind the rendering drain grate in the plant's blood-processing basement. Per Hound rules the entrance moves as it hunts: it can reappear in a burnt-out delivery truck, a junkyard compactor, or a drainage canal near whichever Grudge target is being stalked.
- **Architectural Manifestation**: A barely coherent hellscape of whipping scalding wind, boiling rain, and licking flame over a bare floor of slick tile. Meat hooks swing from nothing on endless overhead rails. A single conveyor belt runs through the whole place and never ends.
- **Atmospheric Sensory Tags**: *Hellish, Gory, Barren, Inhospitable, Ferocious, Chaotic.*
- **Palace Rules**:
  - *Outside Palace*: If The Kill Floor takes 4 slashes or is reduced to 0 slashes outside its Palace, it retreats through the nearest drain or wound in the world until Pressure increases, healing 1 (or 2d3 if full). It can **never** be executed outside its Palace.
  - *Inside Palace*: Fighting here initiates a climactic Execution Scene where the Sin cannot flee.

### Grudge Targets
| # | Target | Status | Notes |
|---|--------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Gordon Pruitt, plant manager | Alive | Hiding in a lakeside rental, drinking, armed with a hunting rifle. |
| 2 | Alma Thorne, safety inspector | Alive | Working normally, unaware she is on a list. Lives with her teenage daughter. |
| 3 | Dr. Wendell Cray, company physician | Alive | Running a private clinic downtown. Has already locked his doors. |

### Escalation Track (0 to 6)
- **Pressure 0-2 (Hunt Begins)**: Strays and pets are found torn open. Meat in nearby shops and butcher counters spoils overnight. Townsfolk feel watched and stop walking alone after dark.
- **Pressure 3-4 (The Hound Hunts)**: Each increase, the Hound goes after one living target. Exorcists present can intercept in a conflict scene; if absent, the target is slaughtered messily. Local police cordons collapse into panic.
- **Pressure 5 (Open Slaughter)**: With targets dead or hunted, collateral deaths mount: coworkers, family, cops. If all three are dead, the Hound adds an NPC the Exorcists have met as a new target.
- **Pressure 6+ (Out of Control)**:
  - The Kill Floor gains **+1 Category** (CAT 4, Execution Talisman 10 segments before Domain 2 bonuses).
  - Every NPC the Exorcists have met, and the Exorcists themselves, are added to its Grudge. The whole district becomes its hunting ground.

## 5. The Three Chosen Domains

### Domain 1: The Shadow on the Line
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[03_III_Hound#3. The Catching of the Doe|3. The Catching of the Doe]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: A hunched silhouette at the edge of a fluorescent flicker, apron strings snapping in a draft that has no source, a rhythmic click behind a closed door.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - At the start of the hunt the Admin picks one Exorcist as the stalked target.
  - *Once a scene*, in any scene, the Admin may declare the target glimpses the Hound following them (real or not). They take 1 stress, which cannot cause an injury. The Admin can do this **3 times total** per hunt.
  - In any conflict scene the Hound gets a **free reaction** at the start (roll the risk die as normal), aimed at the stalked Exorcist if possible.

### Domain 2: Feeding the Grudge
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[03_III_Hound#5. The Fattening of Rage|5. The Fattening of Rage]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: Every kill leaves it denser, redder, and hotter. Fresh blood soaks into the apron and the knives grow thicker and more crowded along its arms.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - *Once a scene*, if it slays any mundane human as part of a reaction, it heals 1 segment on its Execution Talisman.
  - If it has slain at least one of its Grudge targets, its Execution Talisman grows by **+2 segments**.
  - If it has slain all of its original Grudge targets, it inflicts **+1 stress** with all reactions.

### Domain 3: The Cold Room's Weight
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[03_III_Hound#7. The Measured Weight of Death|7. The Measured Weight of Death]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: Folk superstition from Lola's grandmother: warm meat does not die, only the cold keeps the dead down. Its hide lets flame, lead, and steel slide off it like grease.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - The Hound takes **-1 slash** on its Execution Talisman.
  - This is removed for one scene when Exorcists expose it to its weakness: **Extreme Cold** (a blast freezer, liquid nitrogen, an industrial cold room such as the plant's walk-in freezer 2).

## 6. Combat Profile & Execution Talisman

### Execution Talisman: 9 Segments (6 + CAT 3)
`[ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ]` (+2 segments if any original Grudge target is slain)

### Reaction Die Ladder (Derived from Opponent's Risk Die)
- **Result 1 (Severe Reaction)**: 3 Stress (or major Complication / Threat trigger)
- **Result 2-3 (Moderate Reaction)**: 2 Stress (or Complication / Threat)
- **Result 4-6 (Minor Reaction)**: 1 Stress

### Attacks & Armament
- **Attacks with**: Boning-knife forearms, needle teeth, rusted hook-chain, blood-soaked apron strips, scalding steam.

### Tension Moves (Director Guidelines)
1. *Send Minions / Traces* (Cleaver Myrmidons rise from floor drains)
2. *Ambush Exorcists*
3. *Involve Authorities*
4. *Separate Someone*
5. *Force a Difficult Choice* (protect a Grudge target, or the bystanders in the way)
6. *Escalate the Situation*
7. *Start / Progress a Ticking Clock* (Hound closing on the next target)
8. *Activate a Domain*
9. *Threaten or Twist an NPC*
10. *Introduce a New Obstacle*

### Threats (1 per round)
1. *Cause massive collateral damage by bulldozing through a building to reach a Grudge target.*
2. *Attempt to tear someone in half.*
3. *Hurl an Exorcist through a roof or wall.*
4. *Spit torrents of boiling blood.*
5. *Cut everything within reach into ribbons.*
6. *Inflict a hook: sink a meat hook into someone and drag them behind it.*

### Complications (Max 3 per conflict scene)
1. *Move faster than the eye can see (all actions to hit it are Hard until it is slowed).*
2. *Set everything on fire with flailing, scalding blood.*
3. *Give off massive steam (visibility drops; ranged actions Hard).*
4. *Become enraged: expands with additional blades.*
5. *Add a bystander to the scene as a hostage or target.*

### Severe Attack: Apoplexy (Flayed Skin) (Once per Hunt)
- **Trigger**: Result of '1' on an Exorcist's Risk Die.
- **Participation**: Target exorcist and any allies who fly to their aid (those who do not offer aid cannot participate).
- **Dice Pool**: Starts at 5d6. Subtract 1 die for each affirmative answer. If an answer is 'no', the Exorcist or a helper may immediately make a single action roll to fix it, with only moments to act, suffering consequences as normal if they fail:
  1. *Do you have a sword (or something like it)?*
  2. *Are you calm, collected, and focused?*
  3. *Do you have a shield (or something like it)?*
  4. *Is The Kill Floor hindered, distracted, or under duress in some way?*
- **Consequence Ladder**: The Hound separates the chosen Exorcist from the group (hurls them through a wall, into the Palace, or off an overpass), then attacks with immeasurable fury. Each die rolled is one attack.
  - For every die rolled, the Exorcist and anyone aiding them gain **1 stress**, no matter what.
  - For every '1' rolled, the target suffers **2 additional stress** and has a piece of skin cut away, causing permanent scarring. Roll 1d6: 1 Face or ears, 2 Scalp, 3 Arms, 4 Legs, 5 Chest, 6 Back.
  - Or improvise: make something Hard or Risky.

## 7. Traces (Minions & Offshoots)

### Trace Name: Myrmidons (Cleaver Myrmidons)
Shuddering, half-formed echoes of the Hound that rise from the blood and meltwater in floor drains, wearing scraps of plant uniform. They disintegrate in a few hours.
- **Execution Talisman**: 2 (Solo) | 4 (Group) | 6 (Massive Group)
- **Traits & Immunities**:
  - Deal **+1 stress** to Exorcists who already have an injury.
  - Rip Exorcists in half when inflicting injuries if it is the last injury, causing instant death instead of putting them on the brink of death.
- **Reactions**:
  - *Inflict Stress*: Attacks with teeth, claws, ear-splitting screaming. (1): 3 stress, (2-3): 2 stress, (4+): 1 stress.
  - *Complications & Threats*: Grow spines, rake with claws, snap bones in jaws, grow a new limb or head.
  - *Bloody Steam (1-2), Complication*: Steam enters the lungs of nearby Exorcists. They take 1 stress before they can speak or perform strenuous activity (running, climbing).

## 8. Specific Afflictions

1. **Blood Rage**: Afflicted Exorcists roll **+1D** when inflicting harm, violence, or physical force, but must take 1 stress to take any other type of action.
2. **Ganglia Fever**: Afflicted Exorcists are feverish and hot, and roll **one less resting die**.
3. **Blood Scent**: When any Exorcist suffers an injury, you may gain a psyche burst. If you did this at least once during a mission, permanently add to your agenda: **taste blood.**
