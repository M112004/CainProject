# SIN_TOAD_TASTING_MENU

> **Codename / Designation**: Subject T-308 / "THE TASTING MENU"  
> **Type**: V. TOAD  
> **Form**: Type II (Fused with Host)  
> **Category (CAT)**: CAT 3  
> **Primary Emotion**: Indulgence  
> **Host**: Desmond "Dez" Ferreira (Age 29, former kitchen porter at the luxury restaurant Maison Aurelle, evicted, owed roughly 14,000 in withheld wages, whereabouts unknown)  
> **Canonical Reference**: [[05_V_Toad]] | [[Designing_Sins]]

## 1. Lore & Manifestation Background

### Inciting Incident & Host Relationship
Dez washed pots at Maison Aurelle for four years, working doubles on a "training wage" that chef-owner Aurelio Marchetti never converted to a real contract. When his younger sister Inês developed type 1 diabetes, the insulin cost more than his rent. He asked for his withheld wages. Marchetti laughed and told him the exposure was payment enough. Three weeks later Inês went into ketoacidosis in a rented room while Dez was scrubbing grease traps at a twelve-course charity gala for the new wing of St. Brannock's Hospital, a night where the guests ate truffle and gold leaf and the pharmaceutical sponsors toasted "access to care."

He came home to her body. Two days later the Toad climbed out of his throat laughing. It does not grieve. It is hungry for him, and for everything he was told he could not have. It is fond of Dez, in the way a loud uncle is fond of a nephew who will finally eat.

### Appearance & Psychophysiology
Dez has grown wide and pale, his jaw loosened, his tongue long and prehensile enough to snatch a tray from across a room. Skin is damp with a shine like glazed pastry. He moves in sudden bounds and squeezes through service hatches and dumbwaiters without effort. The mouth opens far past anatomy, a velvet-lined dark that smells of carpet cleaner and expensive cologne, with a faint undertone of truffle and burnt sugar. Gold flecks have settled in his eyelashes.

### Behavior & Combat Psychology
Charming, quick, theatrical. The Tasting Menu greets Exorcists with a waiter's bow and a menu of things it intends to take from them. It loves to talk, to joke, to pour wine it just stole, and it gets cruel only when bored or rushed. It treats Dez like a reluctant guest of honor, shoving him toward the table and mocking him when he hesitates. It abandons banter the instant someone mentions Inês by name.

### Specific Sin Limitations & Weaknesses
- **Vain and Talkative**: Prefers verbal sparring and gloating to fleeing. A good insult or a flattering lie buys time.
- **Greedy and Impatient**: Can be baited by physical or conceptual wealth. A tempting prize or a "rare vintage" makes it break formation.
- **Mobility-Dependent**: Relies on leaping, squeezing, and gullet capacity. Sealing its routes and overfilling its maw hurts it badly.
- **The Line**: It will not take from the sick or the dying. Medical patients, hospice residents, and children's medicine are off its menu, which Exorcists can exploit as cover.

## 2. Traumas (The Three Canonical Questions)

> *Rule: These three questions must NEVER be modified in wording. Uncovering the truthful answer during the hunt allows Exorcists to **Counter** a reaction once per question, rolling 1d3 to reduce stress and deal that many slashes to the Execution Talisman.*

1. **What do you deserve that was denied to you?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: His withheld wages and the months of insulin they would have bought, which would have kept Inês alive.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: A payroll ledger kept by Dez in a spiral notebook, tallying unpaid hours down to the minute, and an unsent wage-theft complaint with a lawyer's card clipped to it.

2. **While you were starving, who was feasting?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: Aurelio Marchetti, his gala guests, and the pharmaceutical sponsors of St. Brannock's new wing, all eating a twelve-course menu on the night Inês died.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: Gala menu cards and sponsor placards in the Maison Aurelle archive, with the sponsor name circled in the host's handwriting, and a staff-schedule printout showing Dez clocked in until 2 AM that night.

3. **Where do you draw the line?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: It refuses to take from the sick or the dying. Every time it stole medicine, it returned the portion that belonged to a patient. Dez will not become a thief of the helpless.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: A child's insulin pen found on the front step of a pediatric clinic with a gold-leaf thumbprint on the cap, and a security tape showing the Toad handing back a hospice resident's oxygen tank.

## 3. Palace (The Pocket Dimension)

- **Location in Investigation Zone**: Behind the walk-in pantry door of Maison Aurelle's basement kitchen, a door that is always slightly warm and smells of perfume.
- **Architectural Manifestation**: A sprawling resort-casino that mimics the gala venue, scaled to absurdity: marble foyers, buffet tables that never empty, chandeliers hung with stolen jewelry, a vault wing packed with gold bullion, cars, and gutted catering trucks. Dez's grey kitchen-porter apron is pinned in a glass case at the center like a relic.
- **Atmospheric Sensory Tags**: *Opulent, Gaudy, Glitzy, Spacious, Extravagant, Luxurious, Comfortable.*
- **Palace Rules**:
  - *Outside Palace*: If The Tasting Menu takes 4 slashes or is reduced to 0 slashes outside its Palace, it leaps into the nearest service hatch or vent and retreats to the pantry door until Pressure increases, healing 1 (or 2d3 if full). It can **never** be executed outside its Palace.
  - *Inside Palace*: Fighting here initiates a climactic Execution Scene where the Sin cannot flee.

### Escalation Track (0 to 6)
- **Pressure 0-2 (Appetite)**: High-worth but mundane thefts. Cash from tills, luxury cars from valet stands, wine cellars, medicine from pharmacies (but never from patients), couture from boutiques.
- **Pressure 3-4 (Excess)**: Unreal amounts of the above. Entire dealerships, a bank vault, the contents of every cold-storage warehouse on the waterfront. Prices spike, shelves empty, fear of shortages grips the district.
- **Pressure 5 (Gluttony)**: It swallows whole stores and restaurants, staff included, along with yachts, buses, and local celebrities. Marchetti and his gala guests are prime menu items.
- **Pressure 6+ (Out of Control)**:
  - The Tasting Menu gains **+1 Category** (CAT 4, Execution Talisman 10 segments).
  - It can steal **conceptual or intangible items**: abstract wealth, stocks in a company, light, artistic skill, happiness. Entire neighborhoods lose their appetite for food, joy, or colour.

## 5. The Three Chosen Domains

### Domain 1: Truffle Oil and Rendered Fat
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[05_V_Toad#2. Greasing the Palms|2. Greasing the Palms]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: A wave of expensive-smelling, glistening grease and glazed syrup that pours from the Toad's mouth, smelling of truffle and caramelized onion.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - As a **complication** or **threat**, the Toad vomits a thick, nauseating slime that reeks of expensive perfume and carpets an area about the size of a large room. It chooses whether the slime is incredibly **sticky** or incredibly **slippery**.
  - Actions that require concentration, quick movement, or manual dexterity become **Hard** in the area.
  - Any action that requires moving around by the Exorcists rolls **two risk dice and picks the lowest**.

### Domain 2: The Staff Meal
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[05_V_Toad#7. Keeper of the Ludic Menagerie|7. Keeper of the Ludic Menagerie]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: Waiters, sommeliers, and line cooks in impeccable uniforms step from its mouth with blank smiles, carrying keys, trays, and cleavers.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - The Toad stores brainwashed humans in its gullet. They are freed with no memory of the incident when the Toad is defeated.
  - It can pull out a human to perform any mundane servile task (cook, clean, drive), always arriving with something convenient to the situation (a guard with a door key, a taxi driver).
  - As a **reaction (1-3)** in a conflict scene, it pulls out a goon: creates a **Toadspawn** trace with Execution Talisman 1, or adds +1 to an existing trace's talisman.
  - As a **threat** in any conflict scene, it kidnaps a mundane human or NPC present and adds them to its collection.

### Domain 3: Gold Leaf on Everything
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[05_V_Toad#8. The Great Glittering Adversary|8. The Great Glittering Adversary]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: Whatever it touches blushes with gold dust and hardens into a gilded dessert-sculpture of itself, down to the crumbs.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - By touching any surface or living matter, it transforms it into gemstone or gold. It uses this as a **threat** or **complication** to inflict stress, block the Exorcists, or inflict **Midan Touch**.
  - **Midan Touch**: An arm or leg of the afflicted Exorcist slowly turns to gemstone or rock, usually starting from the contact point. Actions requiring speed or dexterity become Hard. After each rest the afflicted Exorcist takes 3 stress; if this stress would inflict an injury, they instead suffer instant death and turn into a jeweled statue.

## 6. Combat Profile & Execution Talisman

### Execution Talisman: 9 Segments (6 + CAT 3)
`[ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ] [ ]`

### Reaction Die Ladder (Derived from Opponent's Risk Die)
- **Result 1 (Severe Reaction)**: 3 Stress (or major Complication / Threat trigger)
- **Result 2-3 (Moderate Reaction)**: 2 Stress (or Complication / Threat)
- **Result 4-6 (Minor Reaction)**: 1 Stress

### Attacks & Armament
- **Attacks with**: Crushing punches and kicks, muscular tongue, pressurized blood spray from the eyes (short range), a flung carving cleaver, hot sauce.

### Tension Moves (Director Guidelines)
1. *Send Minions / Traces* (Maison staff and private security as Toadspawn)
2. *Ambush Exorcists*
3. *Involve Authorities*
4. *Separate Someone*
5. *Force a Difficult Choice*
6. *Escalate the Situation* (a bigger theft: an entire block's worth)
7. *Start / Progress a Ticking Clock*
8. *Activate a Domain*
9. *Threaten or Twist an NPC* (swallow Marchetti's family)
10. *Introduce a New Obstacle*

### Threats (1 per round)
1. *Summon bodyguards from the gullet.*
2. *Steal something from the Exorcists (pockets, gear, a cherished keepsake).*
3. *Collapse or hurl a chandelier, vehicle, or part of the building.*
4. *Set off a bomb that was pre-swallowed.*
5. *Swallow someone whole.*
6. *Inflict a hook: pull an Exorcist off balance with its tongue.*

### Complications (Max 3 per conflict scene)
1. *Leap out of reach on muscular legs or squeeze into a service hatch.*
2. *Entangle the arena in traps (loose wires, tablecloth snares).*
3. *Trigger security alarms or lock all doors.*
4. *Vomit slime or disgorge stomach contents.*
5. *Reveal hidden explosives in a cake, wine crate, or sculpture.*
6. *Add a bystander (a terrified waiter or guest).*

### Severe Attack: Grand Finesse (Once per Hunt)
- **Trigger**: Result of '1' on an Exorcist's Risk Die.
- **Participation**: Target exorcist and any allies who fly to their aid (those who do not offer aid cannot participate).
- **Dice Pool**: Starts at 5d6. Subtract 1 die for each affirmative answer. If an answer is 'no', the Exorcist or a helper may immediately make a single action roll to fix it, with only moments to act, suffering consequences as normal if they fail:
  1. *Are you accepting of your powers?*
  2. *Are your allies close enough to touch you skin to skin?*
  3. *Are you willing to part with your kit?* (If yes, the Toad is distracted by stealing every ticked item of gear. They disappear until the Toad is defeated.)
  4. *Is The Tasting Menu hindered, distracted, or under duress in some way?*
- **Consequence Ladder**: Roll the dice.
  - The Exorcist and anyone aiding them take **1 stress per die rolled**, no matter the result.
  - If **at least one '1'** comes up, the Toad steals the **ability to use psychic powers** from the targeted Exorcist. These coalesce into a **psychic shadow**, a Sin with Execution Talisman **3** that uses reactions to flee. If it is destroyed, captured, or the scene ends, it fuses with its host again, ending the effect.
  - If **two or more '1's** come up, the shadow's Execution Talisman is **5** instead.
  - Or improvise: make something Hard or Risky.

## 7. Traces (Minions & Offshoots)

### Trace Name: Toadspawn (Maison Staff & Private Security)
Humans drawn by the promise of the wealth the Toad accumulates: Marchetti's bodyguards, line cooks on the Toad's payroll, a hired security van. Oblivious, brainwashed, or willfully ignorant of what their paymaster is.
- **Execution Talisman**: 1 (Solo) | 3 (Group) | 5 (Large Group)
- **Traits & Immunities**:
  - Mundane humans armed with mundane weaponry, no less deadly.
  - Often have access to cars or security vans.
- **Reactions**:
  - *Inflict Stress*: Attacks with firearms, close quarters combat, kitchen knives. (1): 3 stress, (2-3): 2 stress, (4+): 1 stress.
  - *Complications & Threats*: Fire a stun gun, charge an Exorcist, pin down with a barrage of gunfire, get in a vehicle, throw a grenade.
  - *Call Reinforcements (1), Threat*: Someone gets on the radio or punches an alarm. Add 1d3 to the Execution Talisman if allowed to follow through.

## 8. Specific Afflictions

1. **Midan Touch**: An arm or leg slowly becomes gemstone or rock. Actions that require speed or dexterity are Hard. After each rest, take 3 stress; if this would cause an injury, instant death and turn into a jeweled statue.
2. **Starvation**: All actions are Hard until you or an ally mark 1 KP and allow you to eat something. The effect resets and you must eat again after you rest.
3. **Itchy Fingers**: Once a scene, stealing something (anything) gives you 1 psyche burst and 1d3 sin. Permanently add to your agenda: **steal.**
