> **Category (CAT)**: CAT 3  
> **Primary Emotion**: Desire  
> **Host**: Priya Nandakumar (Age 38, former understudy soprano at the Verlaine Opera House, now founder of the "Sing With Me" community choir and livestream, unmarried, no living family)  
> **Canonical Reference**: [[02_II_Idol]] | [[Designing_Sins]]

## 1. Lore & Manifestation Background

### Inciting Incident & Host Relationship
For eleven seasons Priya covered the title roles at the Verlaine Opera House. She knew every part by heart. She sat in the wings in full costume every night, and in eleven years the star soprano Celestine Marlowe never missed a single performance. Priya never once went on. When Marlowe finally retired, the board hired a famous guest soprano from abroad for the farewell season instead of promoting the understudy. Priya was told, kindly, that she had "the voice of a very good chorus member."

She stopped singing for a year. She took a job selling vocal supplements by phone. Then a lonely night, a bottle of wine, and a whispered lullaby into an empty apartment called the Idol. It did not tear out of her. It leaned in, kissed her forehead, and said, "They never deserved you." Priya loves it. She believes it is her real voice finally getting its turn.

### Appearance & Psychophysiology
The Idol is Priya as she always imagined herself: taller, poised, luminous skin, long dark hair that never frizzes, and a face with nothing mean in it. It wears a plain black concert gown that shifts to a different decade of costume each time you blink. Her voice is warm and effortlessly resonant, and graceless humans cannot stop themselves from leaning toward it. Under strain the gown splits into hundreds of paper-thin stage-curtain tendrils and the face opens into a wide, singing mouth ringed with applause-like clicking. Rooms smell of rosin, powder, and lilies.

### Behavior & Combat Psychology
Generous, funny, tender, and utterly unwilling to share a stage. The Understudy remembers every name, birthday, and wound of its followers and weaponizes each. It begs, flatters, sulks, and offers a better life to anyone who listens. It cannot bear being interrupted, ignored, or upstaged, and it tolerates no other voice at its performances. It becomes cold and surgical the moment someone claims Priya was merely adequate.

### Specific Sin Limitations & Weaknesses
- **Visible to the Graceless**: Anyone can see it, so it can be photographed, filmed, and challenged on a public stage.
- **Cult Dependent**: Its safety and its power rest on its devotees. Isolated from them it is deeply vulnerable.
- **Vanity**: It cannot resist a spotlight, an audience, or the chance to correct a slight. Exorcists can bait it with any stage.
- **Unlovable Core**: Confronting the truth behind its third trauma shakes both host and Sin, leaving it psychically fragile.

## 2. Traumas (The Three Canonical Questions)

> *Rule: These three questions must NEVER be modified in wording. Uncovering the truthful answer during the hunt allows Exorcists to **Counter** a reaction once per question, rolling 1d3 to reduce stress and deal that many slashes to the Execution Talisman.*

1. **What is your dream?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: To walk on stage as the lead, just once, and be applauded by an audience that came to hear her and not someone else.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: A cracked mirror in her old dressing room covered in handwritten curtain-call speeches, each dated to a night she covered and did not perform.

2. **Why did you give up on your dream?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: After Marlowe's retirement the board hired a guest star, and the chorus master told Priya, in front of the company, that she would "never carry a house." She decided hoping was the humiliation.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: A board-meeting memo from the Verlaine Opera House archive that rejects "internal casting" with her name misspelled, and a voicemail from the chorus master she saved and replayed hundreds of times.

3. **Why do you think you are incapable of being loved?**
   - **Truthful Answer**: Everyone who ever said they loved her loved a performance: her father only listened when she sang, and her ex-fiancé said he loved "the girl in the wings, not the girl at the table." She believes the real Priya is uninteresting and that only the role is lovable.
   - **Investigation Clue Vector**: Birthday cards from her father addressed to "my little songbird," none mentioning her name, and an unsent letter to her ex-fiancé reading: "If I stop performing, will you still stay?"

## 3. Palace (The Pocket Dimension)

- **Location in Investigation Zone**: Hidden behind the false proscenium door of the "Sing With Me" rehearsal hall in the Verlaine's disused east wing, next to the community choir's cardboard sign.
- **Architectural Manifestation**: A gilded, impossible opera house with no exterior walls. Endless horseshoe tiers of velvet boxes rise into a painted sky. Every seat is filled with a silent, applauding audience. A single spotlight tracks the Idol across a stage that stretches to the horizon.
- **Atmospheric Sensory Tags**: *Luxurious, Gilded, Airy, Captivating, Impressive, Gaudy, Holy.*
- **Palace Rules**:
  - *Outside Palace*: If The Understudy takes 4 slashes or is reduced to 0 slashes outside its Palace, it slips through the nearest stage door and retreats to its Palace until Pressure increases, healing 1 (or 2d3 if full). It can **never** be executed outside its Palace.
  - *Inside Palace*: Fighting here initiates a climactic Execution Scene where the Sin cannot flee.

### Escalation Track (0 to 6)
- **Pressure 0-2 (A Few Dozen)**: The choir has about thirty devoted members, mostly lonely adults. The "Sing With Me" livestream hits tens of thousands of views. Each time pressure rises, the Admin secretly adds an NPC the Exorcists have met to the cult.
- **Pressure 3-4 (Standing Ovation)**: Hundreds of followers. Concert halls, hospital wards, and nursing homes host free "healing recitals." Local media praise her. Former cult NPCs now act on her psychic directives and quietly work against the Exorcists.
- **Pressure 5 (Sold Out)**: Thousands follow her online and in person. Opera board members, local officials, and a regional politician appear at private performances. Detours and denials block CAIN investigators.
- **Pressure 6+ (Out of Control)**:
  - The Understudy gains **+1 Category** (CAT 4, Execution Talisman 10 segments).
  - It adds **every NPC in the mission area to its cult**, including any NPC the Exorcists meet in the future.

## 5. The Three Chosen Domains

### Domain 1: The Chorus Takes the Pain
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[02_II_Idol#3. Hold My Darlings|3. Hold My Darlings]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: Cultists sway in unison behind her; when she flinches, a chorister collapses clutching their own throat.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - *Once a scene*, when the Idol would take slashes on its Execution Talisman from an Exorcist's action, it can transfer the harm to any cultist or group of cultists present in the scene instead. This includes NPCs added to its cult.
  - The Idol sees through the eyes of any cult member as if they were its own and speaks through their mouths with its own voice.

### Domain 2: The Unspeakable High Note
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[02_II_Idol#6. Taking the Ears|6. Taking the Ears]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: A soaring, harmonic voice that makes the air itself bend, followed by a single impossible syllable that rings in the skull like struck crystal.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - As a **threat** or **tension move**, the Idol speaks to a group of mundane humans. If successful, they are added to the cult as a group of cultists. If cultists are already present, increase their Execution Talisman by 2 instead.
  - As a **threat**, the Idol begins to speak an unspeakable word. If successful, one Exorcist who hears it takes 2 stress and the **deafened** affliction for the rest of the mission (cannot hear; activities reliant on hearing are Hard or impossible). Already deaf characters are immune. At the end of the mission, roll a 1d6 fortune: on a 1 or 2, the character is **permanently deafened** but has time to adjust, with no further deleterious effects.

### Domain 3: The Curtain Call
- **Based on Canonical Domain**: [[02_II_Idol#7. The Glory|7. The Glory]]
- **Thematic Visual Flavor**: The gown unfurls into a hundred stage curtains lit from within. A halo of golden follow-spots blinds the room and the audience falls to its knees.
- **Mechanical Rules**:
  - *Once a scene*, the Idol takes its glorious form as a **complication**. Non-cultist humans witnessing the form instantly become unconscious. Exorcists witnessing it either take 1 stress when acting against the Idol and ignore it, or avert their gaze (making anything reliant on sight Hard).
  - Cultists present are motivated and may **re-roll the risk die** when acting, taking the second result as final.
  - One Exorcist sees this form as someone dear to them and must describe who to the group. They take 1 stress when harming the Idol while the form is active.
  - The complication can be dealt with as normal.

## 6. Combat Profile & Execution Talisman

### Execution Talisman: 9 Segments (6 + CAT 3)

### Reaction Die Ladder (Derived from Opponent's Risk Die)
- **Result 1 (Severe Reaction)**: 3 Stress (or major Complication / Threat trigger)
- **Result 2-3 (Moderate Reaction)**: 2 Stress (or Complication / Threat)
- **Result 4-6 (Minor Reaction)**: 1 Stress

### Attacks & Armament
- **Attacks with**: Well-honed opera scissors and costume blades, thin flexible curtain-cord claws, mundane firearms in cultist hands, psychic voice commands.

### Tension Moves (Director Guidelines)
1. *Send Minions / Traces* (choir members as Cultists)
2. *Ambush Exorcists*
3. *Involve Authorities* (a cultist police officer or councilor)
4. *Separate Someone*
5. *Force a Difficult Choice*
6. *Escalate the Situation* (a public recital with a hostage audience)
7. *Start / Progress a Ticking Clock* (the farewell gala)
8. *Activate a Domain*
9. *Threaten or Twist an NPC* (reveal an ally is already a cultist)
10. *Introduce a New Obstacle*

### Threats (1 per round)
1. *Summon cultists from the wings.*
2. *Cause crippling pain with a sustained note.*
3. *Overwhelm the senses with a blinding follow-spot.*
4. *Force an Exorcist to sacrifice something they love.*
5. *Expose a weakness by reciting a secret the Exorcist has told no one.*
6. *Enthrall an Exorcist for a round.*
7. *Take captives from the audience.*

### Complications (Max 3 per conflict scene)
1. *Rile up a crowd until it presses in on the Exorcists.*
2. *Enthrall someone nearby who becomes a hostile voice.*
3. *Blind with glory (see Domain 3).*
4. *Overwhelm with emotion: Exorcists must voice a hidden grief or lose a round.*
5. *Disarm someone with a flattering word.*
6. *Add a bystander (a fan clutching the Exorcist's sleeve).*

### Severe Attack: Marriage (Once per Hunt)
- **Trigger**: Result of '1' on an Exorcist's Risk Die.
- **Participation**: Target exorcist and any allies who fly to their aid (those who do not offer aid cannot participate).
- **Dice Pool**: Starts at 5d6. Subtract 1 die for each affirmative answer. If an answer is 'no', the Exorcist or a helper may immediately make a single action roll to fix it, with only moments to act, suffering consequences as normal if they fail:
  1. *Are you far from the Idol?*
  2. *Do you have love in your life?*
  3. *Does someone aiding you care about you?* (They can describe how.)
  4. *Is The Understudy hindered, distracted, or under duress in some way?*
- **Consequence Ladder**: Roll the dice. The Exorcist and anyone aiding them take **1 stress per die rolled**, no matter the result.
  - If **at least one '1'** comes up, the targeted Exorcist must answer: *Who among your allies do you desire the most?* The answer may be platonic, and the Idol may give them a chance to reconsider.
    - If the answer is "nobody" or "myself," the Idol instantly inflicts an **injury** and knocks the Exorcist unconscious for the remainder of the scene.
    - If the answer is another ally, the Idol **forcibly fuses the flesh of the two together**. No immediate adverse effects, but the pair can only act with teamwork with each other while fused. After they **rest**, they may stay fused or forcibly separate, which inflicts an injury on each. Otherwise the fusion ends after the hunt as CAIN safely separates them. After un-fusing (either way), each takes an agenda item from the other's agenda as a **bold** item.
  - If **two or more '1's** come up, the fusion instead lasts until the end of the hunt and cannot be ended early.

## 7. Traces (Minions & Offshoots)

### Trace Name: Cultists (The Choir)
Mundane humans pulled under the Understudy's Grace: choir members, livestream moderators, ushers, board donors. They behave normally when required but are functionally puppets. Defeating the Idol breaks the spell; they have no memory of their time brainwashed.
- **Execution Talisman**: 2 (Solo) | 4 (Group) | 6 (Large Group)
- **Traits & Immunities**:
  - Mundane humans; no supernatural resistance.
  - Fully and unflinchingly loyal while the Idol lives.
- **Reactions**:
  - *Inflict Stress*: Attacks with bare hands and improvised melee weaponry (music stands, scissors, batons). (1): 3 stress, (2-3): 2 stress, (4+): 1 stress.
  - *Complications & Threats*: Draw unwanted attention, choke someone out, pull out a gun, draw in bystanders, make a shocking reveal.

## 8. Specific Afflictions

1. **Infatuated**: Pick an ally. If you act without their setup or teamwork, you take 1 stress.
2. **Solipsism**: Take 2 stress if participating in teamwork or setup.
3. **Violet Somnia**: You can roll a resting die any time. If you do, you fall asleep until pressure increases and cannot be woken.
