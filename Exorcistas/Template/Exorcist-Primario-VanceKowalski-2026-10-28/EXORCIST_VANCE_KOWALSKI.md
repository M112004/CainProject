## 1. Background & The Five Inquiries (First-Person Voice)

> *Canonical Rule (Rulebook p. 23): Answered in my own words during CASTLE intake processing at North Harbor Sector.*

1. **How did you first manifest your powers?**  
   - *My Response*: On the third level of the naval shipyard drydocks. A rusted gantry crane sheared its mounting bolts and dropped six tons of structural I-beams straight toward four apprentice mechanics. I threw my hands up without thinking. The air between us crystallized into a shimmering, diamond-hard plane of kinetic force with a sound like a freight train locking its brakes. The girders bounced off. Blood exploded out of my tear ducts and my heart felt like it seized on hot gravel.

2. **Is your sin-seed in your brain or in your heart?**  
   - *My Response*: In my heart. When tension fields snap into place, my chest thumps with the heavy vibration of an industrial compressor, and I feel a hard, unyielding knot pressing directly against my aortic arch.

3. **What do you hide in the deepest parts of you?**  
   - *My Response*: I don't shield people because I love humanity. I shield them because when someone dies under my watch, the silence that follows inside my head is so deafening I want to bash my own skull against an iron girder to stop it.

4. **Is your hand your hand?**  
   - *My Response*: My hands are still mine—thick-fingered, scarred by angle grinders, and missing half of my left thumbnail from an old rigging accident. But my legs took the damage when the overflow hit in the sewer conduits: my Achilles tendons stretched into coiled, braided steel wire, and my ankles locked into digitigrade joints. I have to buy work boots two sizes up and cut the heels out.

5. **Do you remember the face of your mother?**  
   - *My Response*: No. Just her wool cardigan draped over the kitchen radiator, the chemical stench of laundry bleach, and the whistle of the shipyard horn at dawn.

## 2. Dossier & Narrative Profile (First-Person Voice)

### Personal Voice & Psychological Evaluation
I know what the CASTLE pencil-pushers write on my medical clearance: *"Subject exhibits pathologically stubborn protective instincts, acute survivor guilt, and a total disregard for personal bodily integrity."* They can evaluate me however they like as long as they issue my ammunition requisitions on time. I don't care about CAIN's theological theories about grace or the metaphysical soul of the world. In my mind, an investigation zone is just an unstable construction site where the scaffolding is actively trying to murder everyone inside. My job is simple: anchor the perimeter, put a slab of kinetic glass between the monster and the kids holding guns, and make sure everybody walks back to the transport van on their own legs.

### Appearance & Sensory Demeanor
Twenty-two years old, broad-shouldered and heavy-set, with short buzz-cut dark hair and bloodshot hazel eyes. I wear a grease-stained CASTLE issue tactical trench over reinforced coveralls, with steel plates bolted across my clavicles and knees. My gait has a slight forward lurch due to my digitigrade tendons. The air within arm's reach of me hums with a faint high-frequency electrical vibration that makes loose pocket change buzz.

### Drive & Immediate Objective
Keep the junior operatives in Section 4 from getting butchered by their own tactical mistakes, drop the Sin before civilian quarantine lines buckle, and avoid letting my Sin count hit double digits again.

## 9. Tactical Synergies & Combat Playbook

### Investigation & Recon Loop
- **Perimeter Elevation**: I use **Conditioning 2** paired with **Preternatural Leap** (1d3 Sin) to bound directly onto fire escapes and high crane gantries, scouting entry points without tripping ground-level tripwires.
- **Silent Kinetic Breaching**: When an entrance is deadbolted or barricaded, I bypass explosives entirely using the **ASP Breaching Bar** (Force 2) or project **Severance** to carve clean rectangular doorways through brick walls.

### Conflict & Execution Loop
- **Opening Move (Round 1 - Zone Partition)**:
  - I drop **Fortress** across the main approach (CAT 3 scale, 10m wide plane with 3 Talisman segments), cutting off the Sin's minions (Traces) and forcing the main target into a narrow kill lane.
- **The Guardian Aegis Loop**:
  - When an ally takes harm, I trigger **Aegis** (*"Can you reach? Yes. Environment shield? Yes. Heart in this? Yes."*), rolling 3d6 to eliminate 2-4 stress.
  - If excess damage threatens an injury, I absorb it via **Iron Wall**.
  - If my talisman drops to 1 stress, **Iron Soul** triggers on a 4+, keeping me on my feet.
  - If an ally does take an injury, **Castle** triggers instantly: I erase 1 stress and gain **+1D** on my next counter-strike.
- **Offensive Climax**:
  - Inside the Palace, I step in with **Severance** (+1D for striking to protect allies) backed by **Blast** and a Psyche Burst, rolling **4D** to cleave through the Sin's defensive carapaces.
