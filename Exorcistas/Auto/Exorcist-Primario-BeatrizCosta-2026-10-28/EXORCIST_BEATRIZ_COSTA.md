## 1. Background & The Five Inquiries (First-Person Voice)

> *Canonical Rule (Rulebook p. 23): Answered directly in my own words during CAIN CASTLE intake screening.*

1. **How did you first manifest your powers?**  
   - *My Response*: I was sixteen and sitting next to my grandfather's bed in Salvador while he took four days to die. On the fourth night I got bored of the rosary and started reading him Drummond out loud, badly. Around the second stanza of "Resíduo" I stopped being in the chair. I was floating by the ceiling fan, looking down at a girl with my braids and a dying man holding her hand. I watched his breath leave. Then I watched it come to me. My aunt found my body cold on the chair at dawn and screamed for a priest, and a priest is how CASTLE found out. That's a very Brazilian way to get recruited.

2. **Is your sin-seed in your brain or in your heart?**  
   - *My Response*: Heart. The doctors at intake were visibly disappointed, they like a clean brain scan to be sorry about. Mine sits against the aortic arch like a swallowed pebble. When I use Jaunt it gets heavy and then it gets very small, and for a second I feel exactly as long as the distance I'm travelling.

3. **What do you hide in the deepest parts of you?**  
   - *My Response*: I'm relieved when hosts die. Not because they were evil. Because a person whose pain has finished is the only kind of person I fully understand. I've told the debrief officers I grieve them, and I do, but under the grief there is a quiet, shameful ease, like setting down a heavy bag. If anyone at CASTLE learns I like the silence afterward more than the rescue, I think they'll stop sending me.

4. **Is your hand your hand?**  
   - *My Response*: Mostly. My right hand and forearm are another matter. After the Centipede hunt the tendons in my forearm started twisting under the skin like wet rope, and the knuckles went a dull gray that doesn't tan. My grip now closes harder than I ask it to. I broke a coffee mug in Recife last spring, apologised to it, and then I put the pieces in my pocket. It still does what I tell it. Most days.

5. **Do you remember the face of your mother?**  
   - *My Response*: I remember her hands better. She braided my hair every Sunday and hummed samba-canção so slowly it sounded like a hymn. The face is gone. When I try to draw it I get my own face, aged, and I stop trying.

## 2. Dossier & Narrative Profile (First-Person Voice)

### Personal Voice & Psychological Evaluation
I write the after-action reports in verse when nobody checks, and the quartermaster has stopped complaining because the verse is accurate. CASTLE calls us assets. I call it a long, well-funded funeral for people who are still walking around. I don't hate them for it. Hating takes more energy than I have left after a hunt.

The people I travel with think I am gentle, and they aren't wrong, but gentle is a tool. I find what a person wants, I name it softly, and then they do what I need. I did it to a terrified councilman in Manaus and I did it to Ostrander, my own squad leader, when I needed him to hold a door. It works because I mean every kind word I say. That is the part that frightens me.

I mourn everyone. The host in the burning basement. The Idol's keeper who begged me to leave him with it. The dead I spoke to through Desecrate, who all answered truthfully and then went quiet again, and the last of whom thanked me. I keep a notebook of their names in the back of my service paperback. Forty-one so far, counting the ones who were only a voice.

### Appearance & Sensory Demeanor
Five-foot-six, narrow, with long braids pinned under the navy CAIN collar so they don't catch in doors. The right sleeve is always buttoned to the wrist and a black leather driving glove covers the hand, in all weather. I smell of clove cigarettes I no longer smoke and of gun oil. A dog-eared Portuguese paperback of Cecília Meireles is clipped inside my jacket. Near me, radios hum a half-tone flat and the temperature drops a few degrees, as if someone just left the room.

### Drive & Immediate Objective
I'm keeping my squadmates alive long enough to bury them properly, which is the only honorable cremation voucher I can afford. Under that, I want to find the Jaunt user who hollowed in Porto Alegre and left an empty shell on a bunk. I need to know if it was quiet.

## 5. Blasphemies & Supernatural Arsenal

- **Blasphemy Strategy**: Hybrid Adaptor
- **Primary Blasphemy**: [[01_Blasphemies_System]]: **Jaunt**
- **Secondary Blasphemy** (Advance #1): **Sympathy** (-1 Sin Cap, +1 advance XP)
- **Passive Benefits**:
  - *Ghostwire (Jaunt)*: I can join my mind telepathically with one other willing person I touch. While within long distance of each other, we can talk telepathically and sense each other's ambient emotional state. It lasts until I use it again, until the other person becomes unconscious, or until either of us closes the connection.
  - *Resonance (Sympathy)*: At the start of the mission I roll on the resonance table (1d3, then 1d6). Using an item I'm resonant with on an action roll gives +1D. I can spend a psyche burst to roll an additional resonance, keep up to three, and only benefit from one at a time. **Mission roll: 32, Hammers.** My father's carpenter's hammer, the only thing I took out of Salvador that isn't paper.

### BLAST Manifestation (All Exorcists)
- *Look & Feel*: A pale, cold-flame silhouette of a hand, translucent like candle wax, leaps off my right knuckles and hits with a sound like a wet, heavy sigh. Radios go quiet around it.
- *Mechanics*: Spend 1 Psyche Burst and roll PSYCHE to unleash a supernatural attack against sins or mundane targets. Scales with CAT.

### Registered Powers (Max 5 Combined)
1. **Threads** (Jaunt, Starting Power):
   - *Tags*: Charm, Self, Until Rest
   - *CAT Scaling*: Sight range is CAT (4).
   - *Mechanics*: Upon using this power I close my eyes and see through my eyelids the patterns the soul leaves in the environment. I can see living beings, even through walls, for CAT range. I can see the traces a sin or someone strong in grace leaves, like a faint trail of light through the air. +1D on actions to track or locate living beings or traces of sins. I cannot see any living matter normally while maintaining it (effectively blind), and actions that rely on sight are hard. It ends when I open my eyes or rest.

2. **Desecrate** (Jaunt, Starting Power):
   - *Tags*: Summon, Adjacent, Instant
   - *CAT Scaling*: None stated.
   - *Mechanics*: I force a semblance of life into a corpse by touching it on the eyes. I may ask the corpse three questions, after which it becomes dead again. It also expires if the hour passes. Can't be used on the same corpse more than once. The corpse answers truthfully, only within its own knowledge and memories, and partial knowledge applies if the head or brain is missing or the body lacks a tongue. I gain or grant +1D when next acting on the answers.

3. **Bond** (Sympathy, Acquired via Advance #3):
   - *Tags*: Self, 1 Scene
   - *CAT Scaling*: None stated.
   - *Mechanics*: For the scene, I bond incredibly tightly with an item I'm holding in one or both hands. I'm resonant with it, I can use it as a cutting or bludgeoning weapon even if it wouldn't normally be a weapon (about the power of a bat, baton, or sword), the item becomes virtually indestructible, and I can recall it to my hand from within short distance.
   - *My Note*: Dad's hammer, usually. It has opinions about Sins.

## 9. Tactical Synergies & Combat Playbook

### Investigation & Recon Loop
- **Primary Method**: Touch a willing teammate for Ghostwire, then use Threads (eyes shut, +1D) to map living beings and Sin traces through walls while the teammate describes the visible world through the link. If a corpse is in play, Desecrate gives three questions and +1D on acting on the answers.
- **Fallback / Leverage**: Burn a Strings favor to make a partner set me up, or spend a psyche burst to roll a new Resonance and hope for a useful object.

### Conflict & Execution Loop
- **Opening Move (Round 1)**: Threads up before the door opens. Ghostwire the squad leader so orders arrive without sound.
- **Synergy Combos**:
  - *Combo A*: Fascination (bonus stress relief when the target rolls a 6 on risk) + Ghostwire (linked to the same teammate) + CZ 75 SP-01 Shadow. I read their emotional state, shoot where they won't be looking, and absorb the stress swings.
  - *Combo B*: Bond (the hammer, resonant, recall within short range) + Force Synergy mark + BLAST. Hammer swings with +1D resonance and +1D Force, and the blast rides the claw.
- **Defensive Contingency**: Strings partners are forced into teamwork with me on a bad round. Brink of death, I Ghostwire out a last warning and give the squad the exit.

## 10. Mission Ledger & Squad Progression Chronicles (First-Person Voice)

> *Squad size is 4-5 operatives. Average Squad CAT must be EQUAL TO OR HIGHER than the Sin's CAT. Missions progress from lower CAT to higher CAT.*

### Mission 1: Operation "Sabiá Preso" (Target: CAT 1 Centipede)
- **Squad Roster (5 Operatives)**: Myself (CAT 1), Ostrander (CAT 2), Mei-Lin Zhou (CAT 2), Dariusz Wilk (CAT 1), Tobi Adeyemi (CAT 1).
  - *Squad Average CAT*: **1.4** >= Sin CAT **1** (Balanced).
- **My Personal Account**: A rail yard outside Campinas, a night janitor whose back had grown too many legs. I had been in CASTLE nineteen days. I held Tobi's hand through Ghostwire because he couldn't stop shaking, and the janitor's grief flooded us both. I forgot I was there to execute. When the Centipede reared, my right hand was already out, empty, as if to take his.  
  The power I used to stop it was not mine. It was too big. I felt the tendons in my forearm braid themselves like a rope. We spared the host. I vomited in a gutter and Wilk lent me his handkerchief and said nothing, which I will love him for until I die.
- **Incidents & Dice Audit**: Sin Overflow. Resistance 1 + 0 marks = 1, control retained, **Sin Mark gained (Arms: Force Synergy)**. Affliction cleared at debrief.
- **Session XP**: +4 XP (Survive, Agenda, Bolded Agenda, Affliction).
- **Outcome & Scrip**: Target Spared (+3 Scrip).
- **Advancement Milestones**: None yet. 4 XP banked (advance not spent until after Mission 2).

### Mission 2: Operation "Casa de Cera" (Target: CAT 2 Ogre)
- **Squad Roster (4 Operatives)**: Myself (CAT 2), Ostrander (CAT 3), Mei-Lin Zhou (CAT 2), Hannelore Voss (CAT 3).
  - *Squad Average CAT*: **2.5** >= Sin CAT **2** (Balanced).
- **My Personal Account**: A farmhouse in Minas Gerais where a widower fed the thing three meals a day because it was shaped like his dead wife. It had eaten two of his neighbors. I got Voss to hold the stairs by promising her I'd read the report aloud to her afterward. She asked for the one about the sea. I kept the promise.  
  We executed it. The man did not thank us. I did not need him to. I touched his wife's eyes before the sheet went over her and I asked her three questions. She answered all of them kindly, which was worse.
- **Incidents & Dice Audit**: Clean run. Hands steady.
- **Session XP**: +4 XP (Survive, Agenda, Bolded Agenda x2).
- **Outcome & Scrip**: Target Executed (+5 Scrip).
- **Advancement Milestones**: 8 XP earned. Advance #1 cashed (4 XP): **Sympathy** acquired as Secondary Blasphemy. Hybrid penalty applied: Sin Cap -1, future advances cost 5 XP. 4 XP banked.

### Mission 3: Operation "Lobo de Vidro" (Target: CAT 2 Hound)
- **Squad Roster (5 Operatives)**: Myself (CAT 3), Ostrander (CAT 3), Mei-Lin Zhou (CAT 3), Tobi Adeyemi (CAT 2), Hannelore Voss (CAT 2).
  - *Squad Average CAT*: **2.6** >= Sin CAT **2** (Balanced).
- **My Personal Account**: A shipping port in Santos, two hundred containers, one Hound that remembered every scent it had ever hunted. Threads painted it through steel plate as a smear of white light, and I led the squad around it for forty minutes like a nervous waltz partner. Adeyemi said I looked peaceful. I was.  
  The host was a customs inspector with a dead son. We spared them both and I recited "Canção" on the radio net until the sirens died out. Voss cried. I did not.
- **Incidents & Dice Audit**: Clean run.
- **Session XP**: +3 XP (Survive, Agenda, Bolded Agenda).
- **Outcome & Scrip**: Target Spared (+3 Scrip).
- **Advancement Milestones**: 7 XP banked after this session. Advance #2 cashed (5 XP): **Strings** (Songbird). 2 XP banked.

### Mission 4: Operation "Santa de Sal" (Target: CAT 3 Idol)
- **Squad Roster (4 Operatives)**: Myself (CAT 3), Ostrander (CAT 4), Mei-Lin Zhou (CAT 3), Dariusz Wilk (CAT 2).
  - *Squad Average CAT*: **3.0** >= Sin CAT **3** (Balanced).
- **My Personal Account**: A chapel in the hills of Ouro Preto, an Idol of black salt that made people stand still and love it. Wilk went first. I felt his devotion through Ghostwire like a hand pressed against my sternum. I had to cut the link before it took me too.  
  Bond and the hammer did the rest. I walked up the aisle with Dad's hammer humming, and I did not look at the face, and I swung. The Idol cracked down the middle with a sound I will hear when I am dying. Ostrander broke the host free. It was a hard victory. I bruised my ribs on the pew and did not feel it until Wilk laughed at me.
- **Incidents & Dice Audit**: Clean run, no overflow, no injury.
- **Session XP**: +4 XP (Survive, Agenda, Bolded Agenda x2).
- **Outcome & Scrip**: Hard Victory (+2 Scrip).
- **Advancement Milestones**: 6 XP banked after this session. Advance #3 cashed (5 XP): **Bond** (Sympathy power). 1 XP banked. **Reached CAT 4** (4 missions survived).
