## 1. Background & The Five Inquiries

> *Canonical Rule (Rulebook p. 23): Answered during initial CAIN containment following the St. Aldric ward blackout.*

1. **How did you first manifest your powers?**  
   - *Answer*: Night shift, ICU, a cardiac arrest I could not reach. Every alarm in the ward was screaming. I wanted it to stop so badly that it did. The monitors went silent, the lights dimmed, and the patient's heart kept beating without a sound. I have never been able to say whether I saved him or only muted him.

2. **Is your sin-seed in your brain or in your heart?**  
   - *Answer*: Heart. When I use it I feel the pulse go soft, as if somebody wrapped a towel around it.

3. **What do you hide in the deepest parts of you?**  
   - *Answer*: That I sometimes prefer the silence to the patients. I have caught myself holding the quiet a few seconds too long, over a bed, before letting the sound back in.

4. **Is your hand your hand?**  
   - *Answer*: Yes. But my fingerprints have gone smooth, like worn glass, and I leave no print on anything I touch. Doctors have stopped asking whether my hands are warm.

5. **Do you remember the face of your mother?**  
   - *Answer*: She worked nights too. I remember her sleeping in the daytime with a pillow over her ears. I remember the pillow better than the face.

## 2. Dossier & Narrative Profile

### Psychological Profile & CASTLE Standing
Specialist Sorensen is a four-mission medical-support operative, rated reliable, soft-spoken, and persistently overextended. Evaluations flag a pattern: she keeps teammates alive past reasonable thresholds and neglects her own recovery. She treats "quiet" as both tool and refuge, and psych notes warn that she may eventually prefer the silence to the people she is saving. She follows CASTLE doctrine on paper. In the field she will break procedure for a wounded civilian without hesitation.

### Appearance & Sensory Demeanor
Thirty-three years old, round-shouldered, with cropped dark hair and tired brown eyes. Left iris is a pale ring of white with a pinprick pupil that glints oddly in dim light. Wears a grey cardigan under her CAIN trench and carries a canvas satchel of dressings. Speaks barely above a whisper. Footsteps make no sound on hard floors, and ambient noise drops within two yards of her.

### Drive & Immediate Objective
Bring everyone home. Secondary: find out if the ICU patient she muted that first night is still alive.

## 9. Tactical Synergies & Combat Playbook

### Investigation & Recon Loop
- **Quiet Entry**: **Blind** on a doorway or alarm panel mutes sound; **Abstract** removes a lock's recognizability so the door will not hold. **Covert 1** and silent footsteps keep Tension still while Surveillance (2D with the Eyes mark) reads the room.
- **Witness Care**: Connection 2 and **Healer** make wounded witnesses talk. Each answered trauma question is a scene she can restore someone to calm.

### Conflict & Execution Loop
- **Opening Move (Round 1 - Dead Air)**: Use **Blind** on the Sin's mouth or the arena's light so allies get +1D to act, or **Abstract** on the enemy's weapon or a trace's vehicle.
- **Defense Combo**: **Hollow** an ally just before a heavy hit (they weigh 1 lb and can be thrown clear), then **Care** to apply a resting die without a rest. **Savior Complex** adds +1D to any action that prevents harm to a human.
- **Offensive Climax**: She is not a damage dealer. When the Sin is cornered in its Palace she uses **Absentia** (+2 CAT) on **Abstract** to strip an Execution-critical object of recognizability, trading a hook for a decisive turn, and then fires **Blast** (PSYCHE 2D + Burst) to finish.

### Mechanical Synergy Analysis
- **Healer + Care + Savior Complex**: Extra resting dice for allies, free resting dice twice a hunt, and +1D for harm prevention. She keeps a team upright through attrition.
- **Smother + Absentia**: Doubles reach on key powers at the cost of a hook. Because hooks reset each mission, she can spend one per hunt on a decisive CAT 6 Abstract.
- **Surveillance Synergy (Eyes)**: Gives 2D to Surveillance from a skill of 1, enough for reading scenes and spotting sin-warped objects.
- **Weakness**: Force 0, Conditioning 0, Authority 0. She cannot hold a line or give orders. She needs a Guardian or Hardline partner to cover her.
