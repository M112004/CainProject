## 1. Background & The Five Inquiries (First-Person Voice)

> *Canonical Rule (Rulebook p. 23): Answered directly in my own words during CAIN CASTLE intake screening.*

1. **How did you first manifest your powers?**  
   - *My Response*: Foster home number four. Mrs. Dolan locked the pantry with a padlock because I stole cereal, and I was nine and hungry and very annoyed. I opened my hand and there was a hole in the air the size of a dinner plate and the pantry was on the other side of it. I ate Froot Loops out of a portal. Then the hole closed on a box of Pop-Tarts and cut it clean in half. Mrs. Dolan screamed. CASTLE showed up in under two days, which is faster than the pizza place.

2. **Is your sin-seed in your brain or in your heart?**  
   - *My Response*: Brain. It feels like a pea of static sitting behind my left ear. When I use Gate it buzzes like a fridge with a bad motor. The doctor said "fascinating" and I said "gross," and we both meant it.

3. **What do you hide in the deepest parts of you?**  
   - *My Response*: I don't know who my dad is, and I made up three different ones to tell the other kids at intake. I also stole a sealed file from the records office about my first foster family and I haven't read it. It's taped under my bunk. I'm scared it's boring. I'm more scared it isn't.

4. **Is your hand your hand?**  
   - *My Response*: Yes, mostly. I have all my fingers and I named them Larry, Moe, Curly, Shemp and Steve (the thumb). Sometimes when I fold space my hand feels like it's been rented out for a second and returned with a small mark on it, like a library book. No Sin Mark yet. The doctors keep checking, and I keep waving at them with Steve.

5. **Do you remember the face of your mother?**  
   - *My Response*: A little. She had a gap in her teeth and she laughed with her mouth closed. She left me a mix CD with "Mom's Songs" written on it. I'm pretty sure the CD isn't Mom's, it's a 2003 Now! compilation. I play it anyway.

## 2. Dossier & Narrative Profile (First-Person Voice)

### Personal Voice & Psychological Evaluation
I'm fourteen, which CASTLE treats as a typo. Everyone older than me on this campus has decided I'm either a mascot or a liability. Fine. A mascot gets snacks and a liability gets a bigger rifle on the squad next to her, so I'm doing great.

Dark jokes are how I keep the screaming in the back of my head from getting out. I name severed parts because it's easier to say "Gerald is over there" than "that's a piece of a person." The Centipede's arm was Gerald. The Lord's glove was Dame Judy. Dr. Aldous asked me in a debrief if I understood the gravity of what I did. I said I'd been weightless since third grade. He didn't write that one down.

I trust my squad about as much as a kid trusts adults who feed her. Which is a lot, and for no good reason, and I'm working on it.

### Appearance & Sensory Demeanor
Four-foot-eleven, wiry, a mess of dark blonde hair held back with a binder clip. The navy CAIN uniform has been taken in at every seam and the sleeves are rolled four times. Bright yellow high-top sneakers I'm not supposed to wear in the field (I do). Around me, loose change slides toward my pockets, and small drafts curl at ankle height where space is thin. I smell like cherry gum and WD-40.

### Drive & Immediate Objective
Pay off the debt I owe Pilar (eleven dollars and a promise). Keep my squad alive so I stop being "the kid" and become "Cass." Read the file under my bunk, eventually. Not today.

## 5. Blasphemies & Supernatural Arsenal

- **Blasphemy Strategy**: Hybrid Adaptor
- **Primary Blasphemy**: [[01_Blasphemies_System]]: **Gate**
- **Secondary Blasphemy** (Advance #1): **Vector** (-1 Sin Cap, +1 advance XP)
- **Passive Benefits**:
  - *Pocket (Gate)*: I fit a compressed tear in space into a piece of clothing I'm wearing. +1 KP. I can stow or retrieve items inside, up to a combined total of 3 KP. Once inside they're stored in extra dimensional space, hidden and safe, no matter their size. The pocket is attached to my clothes and if they are destroyed the items pop out.
  - *Brake (Vector)*: Automatically remove velocity from all projectiles that would hit me, taking -1 stress from them.

### BLAST Manifestation (All Exorcists)
- *Look & Feel*: A crackling, hot-pink bead of ball lightning that spits out of two pinched fingers and goes off with a sound like someone popping a paper bag in a church.
- *Mechanics*: Spend 1 Psyche Burst and roll PSYCHE to unleash a supernatural attack against sins or mundane targets. Scales with CAT.

### Registered Powers (Max 5 Combined)
1. **Tear** (Gate, Starting Power):
   - *Tags*: Summon, Until Rest
   - *CAT Scaling*: Range is CAT (3).
   - *Mechanics*: I create a point in CAT range, and another point within the same range, and I have to see both when I use it. The two points are connected by a slash in the fabric of reality, a portal that can be moved through and connects the two points as though they were right next to each other. Human-size objects, beings and forces can freely move through the tear for the duration, and momentum is preserved.

2. **Pinch** (Gate, Starting Power):
   - *Tags*: Instant, CAT range
   - *CAT Scaling*: Range is CAT (3).
   - *Mechanics*: I choose a single living being or object in CAT range of human size or less. Roll PSYCHE if the target is unwilling, only spending a psyche burst on success. As long as I can see the target, on at least one success I pinch space between us and move it right next to me. To the target the world smears around them. They ignore all physical obstructions between us. I gain or grant +1D when I or any ally next acts to take advantage of this power.
   - *My Note*: Best used on a squadmate who's stuck behind a locked door and is yelling at me.

### Investigation & Recon Loop

## 10. Mission Ledger & Squad Progression Chronicles (First-Person Voice)

> *Squad size is 4-5 operatives. Average Squad CAT must be EQUAL TO OR HIGHER than the Sin's CAT. Missions progress from lower CAT to higher CAT.*

### Mission 1: Operation "Cereal Killer" (Target: CAT 1 Centipede)
- **Squad Roster (5 Operatives)**: Myself (CAT 1), Rourke (CAT 1), Pilar Mendoza (CAT 1), Sung-min Han (CAT 1), Elodie Fabre (CAT 1).
  - *Squad Average CAT*: **1.0** >= Sin CAT **1** (Balanced).
- **My Personal Account**: A grain elevator outside Toledo, in the middle of the night. A farm hand had a thing growing out of his back. Rourke called it "a bug problem" and I said it was a "gap year." I got the squad through a Tear into the silo's upper door and hit the Centipede with the ugliest ball of pink lightning ever filmed. It screamed. I laughed. Then I stopped laughing because it sounded like him.  
  We executed it. I named its arm Gerald and gave it a funeral with a gum wrapper. Sung-min didn't say anything, just handed me a juice box.
- **Incidents & Dice Audit**: Clean run. No overflow.
- **Session XP**: +4 XP (Survive, Agenda, Bolded Agenda, Harm: a scraped shoulder counted as an affliction until debrief).
- **Outcome & Scrip**: Target Executed (+5 Scrip).
- **Advancement Milestones**: Advance #1 cashed (4 XP): **Vector** acquired as Secondary Blasphemy. Hybrid penalty applied: Sin Cap -1, future advances cost 5 XP. Reached CAT 2.

### Mission 2: Operation "Dame Judy" (Target: CAT 1 Lord)
- **Squad Roster (5 Operatives)**: Myself (CAT 2), Rourke (CAT 2), Sung-min Han (CAT 2), Pilar Mendoza (CAT 1), Elodie Fabre (CAT 1).
  - *Squad Average CAT*: **1.6** >= Sin CAT **1** (Balanced).
- **My Personal Account**: A shuttered country club in Kentucky. The Lord was the host's ghost-white butler uniform, standing very still at the end of every hallway. I Pinched Pilar out of a locked ballroom while she was mid-sentence, and she landed next to me and finished the sentence. I'll take that win.  
  Brake took a ricochet off my ribs. I used the Pocket to hide the squad's cash box from the Lord, which isn't in the rules, which is the point. We executed it. I named the glove Dame Judy. It was still twitching.
- **Incidents & Dice Audit**: Clean run. No overflow, no injury.
- **Session XP**: +4 XP (Survive, Agenda, Bolded Agenda x2).
- **Outcome & Scrip**: Target Executed (+5 Scrip).
- **Advancement Milestones**: 4 XP banked (next advance needs 5). **Reached CAT 3** (2 missions survived).
