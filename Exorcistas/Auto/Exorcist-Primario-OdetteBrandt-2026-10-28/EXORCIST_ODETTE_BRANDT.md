## 1. Background & The Five Inquiries (First-Person Voice)

> *Canonical Rule (Rulebook p. 23): Answered in my own words during intake containment following the Harrow Street archive collapse.*

1. **How did you first manifest your powers?**  
   - *My Response*: I was seventeen, working inventory in the sub-basement municipal archives after hours. A burst water main gave out, and the heavy security vault door warped shut. The water was up to my collarbones when my own shadow peeled itself off the damp brickwork and whispered into my ear: *"Third metal shelf from the eastern riser, now."* I dragged myself up. Two seconds later, four hundred pounds of ceiling masonry caved in directly where I had been standing. The shadow giggled.

2. **Is your sin-seed in your brain or in your heart?**  
   - *My Response*: Brain. A small, cold calcification nestled right behind my left optic nerve. Whenever the shadow lies to me or warns of a falling wall, the left side of my skull throbs like an ice pick.

3. **What do you hide in the deepest parts of you?**  
   - *My Response*: The shadow whispered who was going to die in the archive fire two weeks before it happened. I told nobody. I told myself the shadow's predictions were unverified theoretical models. It was a lie. I just wanted to see if the creature could actually see the end.

4. **Is your hand your hand?**  
   - *My Response*: My hands are still mine—thin, scarred by papercuts, and steady enough to hold a surgical scalpel over cold flesh. But my left eye belongs to the dark now. Ever since my overflow during the Black Hound hunt, the sclera went pewter grey and the pupil split into two vertical feline slits. I see every ambient thermal leak and dead soul in the room. Some days I would pay good money to be half-blind again.

5. **Do you remember the face of your mother?**  
   - *My Response*: Yes, perfectly. She passed in an elder-care home outside Leipzig when I was fourteen. She is the only dead person my shadow has never once projected onto a wall.

## 2. Dossier & Narrative Profile (First-Person Voice)

### Personal Voice & Psychological Evaluation
I know exactly what the CASTLE administrative auditors write in my review files: *"Subject displays chronic information hunger, emotional detachment from lethal vectors, and an inappropriate tendency toward levity during biohazard recovery."* Let them write it. When you spend every waking hour listening to an invisible psychic predator murmur casualty statistics into your ear, you either laugh at the absurdity or you put a service round through your palate. I choose to laugh, take notes, and keep my squad breathing. I treat CAIN regulations as a loose suggestion, but I follow forensic protocol to the microgram. If you do not record the Sin's true story, you are just killing meat in the dark.

### Appearance & Sensory Demeanor
Twenty-three years old, slight frame, with dark hair tied back in a messy knot with archival twine. My left eye is pewter with split vertical pupils; when I am in mundane public areas, I wear a folded black linen patch over it. I wear my dark CAIN trench coat unbuttoned, its deep storm pockets stuffed with index cards, grease pencils, forensic chalk, and a brass lighter. The air within two steps of me smells faintly of damp paper dust and cold ozone. When my shadow gets restless, the wall behind me flickers like a failing fluorescent bulb.

### Drive & Immediate Objective
Uncover every buried trauma question before the pressure clock forces an execution roll, and force my shadow to confess what it plans to do with my corpse when my seventh mission bonus runs out.

## 9. Tactical Synergies & Combat Playbook

### Investigation & Recon Loop
- **The Precognitive Truth Siphon**:
  - I open every witness interrogation with **Unveil** to find their core terror, followed immediately by **Dissect** (PSYCHE 3D) to check if they are lying.
  - Both abilities grant **+1D** to squadmates acting on the information, allowing our heavy hitters to crack open leads without risking Tension ticks.
- **The Forensic Surveillance Net**:
  - Combining **Surveillance 4D** (Level 3 + Sin Mark) with **Shiver** allows me to track Sins through sewer grids and abandoned metro tunnels without getting lost.
  - Before kicking open any lair threshold, I cast **Omen** to pre-roll the risk die. If the risk die is a 1, we pull back and approach from another angle.

### Conflict & Execution Loop
- **Opening Move (Round 1 - Threat Neutralization)**:
  - From short range behind cover, I fire **Dissect** or **Omen** to grant +1D to the vanguard striker, then rack the bolt on the **Benelli M4** to suppress approaching Traces.
- **The Moth Recovery Cycle**:
  - Whenever an ally cracks one of the three trauma questions, my **Psyche Jewel** ability triggers automatically: I regain 1 Psyche Burst, clear 1 Sin, and wipe 1 Stress from my talisman.
  - This turns investigative forensic success directly into combat endurance.
- **Offensive Climax**:
  - Once the Sin is cornered inside its Palace, I burn a Psyche Burst to fire **Blast** (PSYCHE 3D + 1D from Surveillance setup = 4D), piercing supernatural defenses alongside squad gunfire.
