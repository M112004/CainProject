## 1. Background & The Five Inquiries

> *Canonical Rule (Rulebook p. 23): Answered during initial CAIN containment after the Kessler Ridge kennel incident.*

1. **How did you first manifest your powers?**  
   - *Answer*: Army K9 handler, night patrol outside a field depot. My dog Ash took a round meant for me and died in my arms. I screamed for her to come back. Something answered from the dark behind my eyes and she stood up again. She was a different shape. She still wagged her tail.

2. **Is your sin-seed in your brain or in your heart?**  
   - *Answer*: Brain. It sits at the base of my skull and pulses when Ash is hungry.

3. **What do you hide in the deepest parts of you?**  
   - *Answer*: Ash did not choose to come back. I did not ask a living thing for permission. I bound her, and every day I tell myself she is happy.

4. **Is your hand your hand?**  
   - *Answer*: Yes, mostly. My right forearm splits along the bone when I call the blade, and after the Kessler Ridge hunt it never fully closed. There is a long pale seam from wrist to elbow that is cold to the touch.

5. **Do you remember the face of your mother?**  
   - *Answer*: Barely. She raised hounds on a farm outside Pécs. I remember her hands more than her face: dry, strong, always smelling of straw and soap.

## 2. Dossier & Narrative Profile

### Psychological Profile & CASTLE Standing
Sergeant Varga (retired from national service) is a four-mission operative rated disciplined, rigidly obedient, and morally conflicted. Evaluators note a narrow pattern: he follows orders to the letter and disobeys only when the order requires him to harm a bound or helpless creature. His loyalty to Ash is the sole variable CASTLE cannot control. Squadmates describe him as the calm voice in a gunfight and the first one to apologize afterward.

### Appearance & Sensory Demeanor
Thirty-nine years old, broad, close-cropped sandy hair, a short beard with grey in it. Wears his CAIN uniform crisp, collar fastened, boots polished. Right sleeve always buttoned to the wrist. Faint smell of gun oil and wet dog. Speaks in short declaratives. His bound sin trails him at short distance, invisible to humans, a large lean shape that leaves no tracks.

### Drive & Immediate Objective
Complete every mission by the book. Secondary: find a way to give Ash a choice, even if it means breaking the one rule he has never broken.

## 5. Blasphemies & Supernatural Arsenal

- **Primary Blasphemy**: [[09_Bind]] — **Bind**
- **Secondary Blasphemy**: None
- **Passive Benefit**:
  - **Sin Binding**: You have the forbidden ability to bind Sins. You have the obedient essence of a minor sin bound to you, under your control.
  - **Bound Sin (Ash)**: Animalistic in form and ability; the wielder decides its form (a large lean hound with black mesh skin and white pupils). It understands language but cannot speak, and is invisible to humans. It follows at short distance, follows simple orders, and uses the wielder's action ratings to do anything. Its general capabilities are CAT 0. If it takes any stress it is banished for the rest of the scene, though the wielder may psychically absorb all stress taken for it instead. In a conflict scene, the wielder may sacrifice their turn to command the sin to act; otherwise it does not act independently.

### BLAST Manifestation
- *Look & Feel*: A choked bark of pressure from the base of his skull. A ghostly hound's head flickers around his fist, snapping out for a half second of bite and crush.
- *Mechanics*: Spend 1 Psyche Burst and roll PSYCHE (2D) for a melee or short-range psychic strike. Supernatural weapon, so not Hard against sins by default.

### Registered Powers (3 of 5 Maximum)
1. **Forbidden Spirit** (Starting Power):
   - *Tags*: Self, 1 scene
   - *Mechanics*: Spend a psyche burst to empower your sin for one scene. The next time it takes action for violence or physical force, it gains +1D. As part of this action, once this scene it can ignore any stress taken. You can also lift limiters on your spirit temporarily, causing a monstrous transformation similar to its original form. It becomes a size equal to CAT and can easily move, lift, or throw objects of equal size.
2. **Hunter Spirit** (Starting Power):
   - *Tags*: Self, 1 scene
   - *Mechanics*: Spend a psyche burst to empower your sin for one scene. The next time it takes action for tracking or observation, it gains +1D. As part of this action it can separate from you up to extreme range when released. It gains flight and extremely good sight and smell, sees in the dark and in the thermal spectrum, and sees clearly up to long range. You can concentrate, dissociating from your body and becoming extremely vulnerable, to use your sin's senses instead of your own. You can engage and disengage this effect at will for the scene.
3. **Horde Spirit** (Acquired via Advance after Mission 3):
   - *Tags*: Self, 1 scene
   - *Mechanics*: Spend a psyche burst to empower your sin for one scene. The next time it takes action for traversal or movement, it gains +1D. As part of this action you can transform it into a vehicle or rideable creature for one scene. It moves at about CAT speed, is partly visible to humans, has room for two passengers, and can glide a short distance in vehicle form, with or without passengers.

## 9. Tactical Synergies & Combat Playbook

### Investigation & Recon Loop
- **Hunter Spirit Sweep**: Varga releases Ash with **Hunter Spirit** to survey a crime scene or perimeter at extreme range, seeing in the dark and thermal spectrum, without raising Tension. He stays in cover, extremely vulnerable but unseen.
- **Horde Spirit Pursuit**: **Horde Spirit** turns Ash into a vehicle or rideable creature for a pursuit or a quick extraction, carrying a second passenger.
- **Chain of Command**: **Black Suit** (+1D) and Authority 2 open doors with police, security guards, and hospital staff. Surveillance and Investigation are modest (1), so he leans on Ash for eyes and on allies for forensics.

### Conflict & Execution Loop
- **Opening Move (Round 1 - Release)**: Spend a Psyche Burst on **Forbidden Spirit**. Ash's next violent action gains +1D and can ignore stress once. If the scene is lethal, lift limiters to give her a CAT 4 size.
- **Defense Combo**: Sacrifice a turn to command Ash. She uses his ratings (Force 1, Conditioning 2) with +1D. If Ash would take stress, Varga absorbs it instead to keep her out of banishment, at the cost of his own stress track.
- **Offensive Climax**: Once the Sin is under half its Execution Talisman, **Eliminate the Stain** (+1D) combines with a Psyche Burst (+1D) and **Bone Blade** (1d3+1 sin, once per mission) for a supernatural melee strike at full damage to Sins.

### Mechanical Synergy Analysis
- **Hardline + Bind**: Black Suit makes him the squad's voice with humans. The sin does the physical work. Mea Culpa relieves sin the first time he takes an injury or affliction, protecting his overflow cap of 9.
- **Bone Blade + Eliminate the Stain**: The blade deals full damage to Sins, and Eliminate the Stain grants +1D once the execution clock is half gone. This is his finishing combo.
- **Weakness**: Negotiation 0, Connection 0, Covert 0. He cannot sneak, persuade, or bond. If Ash is banished (stress taken without absorption) he is left with Force 1 and a service pistol.
