## 1. Background & The Five Inquiries (First-Person Voice)

> *Canonical Rule (Rulebook p. 23): Answered directly in my own words during CAIN CASTLE intake screening.*

1. **How did you first manifest your powers?**  
   - *My Response*: Day of the Dead, I was thirteen, in the Xoxocotlán cemetery with my abuela's whole family fighting about whose turn it was to bring the mezcal. A drunk uncle fell off the wall of a crypt. I screamed and the air in front of me went hard as a glass door, and he bounced off it and rolled into the marigolds without a scratch. Everyone clapped. Abuela crossed herself. She said the dead had been waiting a long time to be rude to him. Eighteen months later, CASTLE found me behind a taquería, holding a stranger's knife in a wall of nothing.

2. **Is your sin-seed in your brain or in your heart?**  
   - *My Response*: Brain. It feels like a grain of rice stuck behind my right eye, which is funny now, because that's the eye that went wrong. The doctors at intake said my scans were "not unremarkable" and my favourite thing in the world is that sentence.

3. **What do you hide in the deepest parts of you?**  
   - *My Response*: I like it. Not the killing. The part right before, when the Sin turns to look at me and I'm the only thing in the room it hasn't decided about yet. I feel awake, like somebody finally turned my volume up. I tell the debrief officers I'm scared. I'm scared of how much I'm not.

4. **Is your hand your hand?**  
   - *My Response*: My hands are fine, thanks for asking. It's the rest of me. My ribs sit wrong under my shirt, a hard ridge of extra bone and gray lesion along my breastbone that I had to stop letting the school nurse see. My right eye turns black in the dark, and the left follows after about a minute, like they're arguing. I can read a menu in a blackout. Nobody has ever hired me to read a menu in a blackout.

5. **Do you remember the face of your mother?**  
   - *My Response*: Yes. That's the problem. She's in Tijuana, she remarried, and she sends me a postcard on my birthday with a new photo of my half-brothers. She has my face. I keep the postcards in a shoebox and I don't answer them.

## 2. Dossier & Narrative Profile (First-Person Voice)

### Personal Voice & Psychological Evaluation
I'm sixteen and I've been to more funerals than weddings, so I make jokes. If I don't laugh at the Ogre's ribcage, I'll hear it instead. So the ribcage is "Don Chuy" and the teeth are "the Reyes family reunion." The squad groans. Good. When they groan, they're not afraid.

CASTLE doesn't love this. Dr. Valenzuela wrote "inappropriate affect" in my file three times. I wrote "inappropriate doctor" in mine once. We're even.

I have three Sin Marks and I'm sixteen. The numbers don't scare me. What scares me is that after the Hound hunt I stood in a parking lot in Utrecht and couldn't remember what my own laugh sounded like. Teodoro sat with me until I did. I don't leave people sitting there when it's my turn.

### Appearance & Sensory Demeanor
Five-foot-three, stocky and sturdy, hair in two thick black braids tied with red yarn. Black sclera in dim light, with a white ring of iris that glows faintly like a dying phone screen. A ridge of gray spurs shows at my collarbone above the uniform. I wear a papel picado charm on my belt, a gift from my abuela. The temperature drops a notch near me, and the air feels slightly thicker, like walking into a closed car in summer.

### Drive & Immediate Objective
To finish my contract and burn the shoebox. Or write back. I flip a coin every Sunday and it changes every week. In the meantime I'm trying to get my squad home to see the next Day of the Dead, mine included.

## 5. Blasphemies & Supernatural Arsenal

- **Blasphemy Strategy**: Hybrid Adaptor
- **Primary Blasphemy**: [[01_Blasphemies_System]]: **Tension**
- **Secondary Blasphemy** (Advance #1): **Flux** (-1 Sin Cap, +1 advance XP)
- **Passive Benefits**:
  - *Iron Soul (Tension)*: When I would lose my last point of stress, roll 1d6. On a 4+, I don't lose any stress and instead remain at 1 stress.
  - *Steal Time (Flux)*: Once a mission, I can reroll all my resting dice for myself or an ally, taking the second result as final.

### BLAST Manifestation (All Exorcists)
- *Look & Feel*: A hard, shimmering disc of marigold-orange force, like a coin spun on its edge, that leaves petal-shaped afterimages in the air.
- *Mechanics*: Spend 1 Psyche Burst and roll PSYCHE to unleash a supernatural attack against sins or mundane targets. Scales with CAT.

### Registered Powers (Max 5 Combined)
1. **Aegis** (Tension, Starting Power):
   - *Tags*: Instant, Short
   - *CAT Scaling*: Short range intervention.
   - *Mechanics*: Once a scene, when I or a visible ally in short range would mark stress from external harm, I can intervene by answering: Can you reach your target in time? Is there any part of the environment you can use to shield your target? Is your heart in this? For each 'yes' I roll 1d6. For every 2+ rolled I reduce stress suffered by 1, and for every 6 rolled by 2. This could reduce stress suffered to 0. If I or an ally gain an injury in the same scene, I regain the use of this power.

2. **Stasis** (Tension, Starting Power):
   - *Tags*: Curse, 1 Scene, Adjacent
   - *CAT Scaling*: None stated.
   - *Mechanics*: With a touch, I can lock myself, a human, or a willing ally in a tension cage that covers them like a second skin, paralyzing them. If a human is hostile or unwilling, roll PSYCHE, and only spend the burst on success. Once trapped, the target is locked in, unable to move or act for the scene, and is immune to all harm and effects from the outside. It only ends once the scene passes and I can't end it earlier. They can be moved around normally like an object.

3. **Reversal** (Flux, Acquired via Advance #2):
   - *Tags*: Adjacent, Instant
   - *CAT Scaling*: Affects an object up to CAT size (5).
   - *Mechanics*: By touching an object up to CAT size, I can reverse its passage through time for the last hour. This could physically move the object, revert damage on an object, etc. It can still affect the physical world, so anything in a reversing object's way would get hit. If it would cause damage or impact, roll PSYCHE for it. It cannot reverse life on non-living matter, such as corpses, but can move them and revert damage. I can stop it by willing it, but resuming requires using the power again.

## 9. Tactical Synergies & Combat Playbook

### Investigation & Recon Loop
- **Primary Method**: Dark Adaptation to scout blacked-out buildings, Surveillance 2 for the second look, and Reversal to rewind a smashed object or a moved piece of evidence from the last hour.
- **Fallback / Leverage**: Steal Time to re-roll rests and get the squad back on its feet faster, buying one more attempt.

### Conflict & Execution Loop
- **Opening Move (Round 1)**: Place myself between the squad and the Sin. Aegis ready.
- **Synergy Combos**:
  - *Combo A*: Iron Soul + Unfeeling Flesh + Conditioning 3D. I'm very annoying to kill. The Sin hits me, I roll for the last point of stress and an injury roll, and I spit a joke.
  - *Combo B*: Rule of Nature (6, 6 adds a slash and a psyche burst) + Stasis + Ka-Bar. Freeze the human guard, then work on the Sin.
- **Defensive Contingency**: Aegis for the ally who is about to fall, and Stasis on anyone who needs to be safe for a scene, myself included.

## 10. Mission Ledger & Squad Progression Chronicles (First-Person Voice)

> *Squad size is 4-5 operatives. Average Squad CAT must be EQUAL TO OR HIGHER than the Sin's CAT. Missions progress from lower CAT to higher CAT.*

### Mission 1: Operation "Sapo Santo" (Target: CAT 1 Toad)
- **Squad Roster (5 Operatives)**: Myself (CAT 1), Teodoro Pike (CAT 2), Ilse Brandl (CAT 1), Hae-won Seo (CAT 2), Marcus Ade (CAT 1).
  - *Squad Average CAT*: **1.4** >= Sin CAT **1** (Balanced).
- **My Personal Account**: A drainage canal near Puebla and a Toad that swallowed whatever people said they wanted. I said I wanted to go home. It spat up a dozen dead fish and a very surprised bus driver. Teodoro called me lucky. I named the Toad's tongue "Don Chuy" and laughed too hard, and Ilse put a hand on my shoulder, and I felt something snap.
- **Incidents & Dice Audit**: Sin Overflow. Die 5 + 0 = 5, control retained, **Mark 1 (Chest, Conditioning Synergy)**. Agenda swapped from BEAST to DOOMED between missions (carried over Rule of Nature and the bolded "Hold Back").
- **Session XP**: +3 XP.
- **Outcome & Scrip**: Target Executed (+5 Scrip).
- **Advancement Milestones**: 3 XP banked.

### Mission 2: Operation "Ofrenda" (Target: CAT 2 Ogre)
- **Squad Roster (5 Operatives)**: Myself (CAT 2), Teodoro Pike (CAT 3), Ilse Brandl (CAT 3), Hae-won Seo (CAT 1), Marcus Ade (CAT 3).
  - *Squad Average CAT*: **2.4** >= Sin CAT **2** (Balanced).
- **My Personal Account**: A seaside town in Oaxaca, my own state, which I did not tell anyone. The Ogre was a fisherman's wife who'd fed her neighbors to a hunger she couldn't explain. I stood in her kitchen and made jokes in Zapotec, and for a second she laughed. Then she ate Hae-won's rifle. We executed it. I walked home along the beach and my ribs hurt for three days.
- **Incidents & Dice Audit**: Sin Overflow. Die 1 (auto-pass) + 1 = 2, control retained, Chest rolled again so the mark evolved: **Unfeeling Flesh**.
- **Session XP**: +4 XP.
- **Outcome & Scrip**: Target Executed (+5 Scrip).
- **Advancement Milestones**: 7 XP earned. Advance #1 cashed (4 XP): **Flux** acquired as Secondary Blasphemy. Hybrid penalty applied: Sin Cap -1, future advances cost 5 XP. 3 XP banked.

### Mission 3: Operation "Pan de Hueso" (Target: CAT 2 Ogre)
