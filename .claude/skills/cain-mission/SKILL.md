# CAIN Mission & Hunt Architect Skill

Use this skill to prepare, structure, or run a CAIN Hunt from briefing to climactic execution.

## The Six Hunt Phases

1. **Phase 1: Briefing**
   - The CAIN handler presents the dossier, anomaly coordinates, known casualties, and initial threat classification.
2. **Phase 2: Arrival**
   - Infiltration into the hot zone, establishing perimeter, securing local contacts or mundane authorities.
3. **Phase 3: Tracking**
   - Following psychic residue, physical gore, distorted electromagnetic phenomena, or missing persons.
4. **Phase 4: Investigation, Preparation, and Rest**
   - **4a. Investigation**: Interrogating witnesses, analyzing forensics, identifying the Sin's host and psychic origin.
   - **4b. Preparation**: Crafting talismans, attuning weapons, fortifying the encounter zone.
   - **4c. Rest**: Clearing stress, treating wounds, coping with Afflictions and Hooks.
5. **Phase 5: Tension and Pressure**
   - The Sin exerts psychic gravity over the environment. Reality cracks. The Director triggers Tension Moves.
6. **Phase 6: Execution**
   - The climactic conflict scene. Unraveling the Sin's Execution Talisman while defending against lethal attacks.

## The Six Canonical Sins

- **I. Ogre**: Raw physical terror, unrelenting brutality, crushing weight, anatomical ruin.
- **II. Idol**: Fascination, mass hypnosis, vanity, aesthetic mutilation, psychic adoration.
- **III. Hound**: Pursuit predator, sensory tracking, relentless ambush, tearing jaws.
- **IV. Centipede**: Infestation, segmented horror, swarming vermin, subterranean terror.
- **V. Toad**: Gluttony, bile, corruptive miasma, acidic secretions, overwhelming inertia.
- **VI. Lord**: Hierarchical dominance, psychic subjugation, marionette thralls, royal blasphemy.

## Procedural Investigation Framework
- **Traumas**: Uncover the human trauma that birthed the Sin to crack open vulnerabilities.
- **Clues**: Ensure 3 distinct paths lead to each critical revelation.
- **Execution Talisman**: Pre-define the 4-8 segments required to make the Sin vulnerable to final execution.
