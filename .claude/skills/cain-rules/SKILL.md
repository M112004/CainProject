# CAIN Rules Adjudication Skill

Use this skill whenever resolving actions, clarifying rules, interpreting edge cases, or structuring conflict in CAIN 1.0.

## Core Resolution Engine

### 1. The Action Roll
- **Dice Pool**: Roll `1d6` + Skill dice (if applicable) + Advantage dice (`+1d6` each). Take the **single highest die**.
- **Results**:
  - **6**: Complete success.
  - **4-5**: Partial success (success with a complication/cost/consequence).
  - **1-3**: Failure or serious complication.
  - **Multiple 6s**: Critical success (Upside + major narrative leverage).

### 2. Roll Modifiers
- **Straight Roll**: Standard situation without extreme peril.
- **Risky Roll**: Failure or partial success imposes severe consequences, damage, or tension escalation.
- **Hard Roll**: Exorcist is at a disadvantage; roll an extra die and take the **lowest** or need higher threshold as dictated by position.
- **Admin Rolls & Fate Rolls**: Used by the Director for luck, timing, or unpredictable outcomes (`1-3` bad, `4-5` neutral/mixed, `6` favorable).

### 3. Divine Agony
- Exorcists can channel the psychic sea within their Flesh.
- Mark **1 Injury / Wound** to re-roll failed dice or force a breakthrough in desperate moments.

### 4. Talismans (Clocks & Seals)
- Talismans represent psychic seals, ticking countdowns, or objective trackers.
- A talisman has a number of segments (typically 4, 6, or 8).
- Exorcists slash segments on success; Sins fill segments when tension or consequences mount.
- An **Execution Talisman** must be unraveled before a Sin can be permanently destroyed.

### 5. Category (CAT Scale)
- Measures supernatural scale and raw dimensional weight (CAT 0: Mundane human, CAT 1-5: Escalating psychic manifestations & Sins).
- **Disparity Rule**: When acting against a target of higher Category without equalizing psychic equipment or a Blasphemy, actions are Hard, mundane attacks inflict negligible effect, and enemy consequences are lethal.

## Ponytail Rules Minimalist Philosophy
- Adjudicate quickly without stalling the narrative.
- Present the odds, state the risk before the roll, and resolve consequences decisively.
